## Input
We need a dashboard for our product team. We care about engagement and revenue. It should be something we look at every week. We have Mixpanel.

## Expected output
Dashboard purpose and audience:
Unclear — "product team" could mean a team of 2 or 20 with very different needs. The dashboard design depends on what decisions it drives.

Metric selection (placeholder until goals are confirmed):
Without knowing the product type or goals, a generic engagement + revenue view would include:
1. Weekly active users (or equivalent engagement metric for the product)
2. Key action completion rate (core engagement event)
3. Revenue or conversions this week vs. last week
4. Churn or cancellation rate (revenue health signal)
5. New user activation rate (conversion from signup to engaged)

These are reasonable starting candidates — but the right metrics depend on what the team needs to decide each week.

Information hierarchy design:
For a weekly review dashboard, a 3-layer structure typically works:
- Layer 1: 2-3 headline numbers (product is healthy / not healthy)
- Layer 2: 3-4 supporting metrics (where is the issue?)
- Layer 3: 2-3 diagnostic metrics (what's causing it?)

Alert thresholds:
Cannot be defined without knowing what "normal" looks like for each metric. Normal ranges are needed before thresholds can be set.

Layout sketch:
Not producible without metric decisions and baseline data.

Metrics to exclude:
Any metric that can't be connected to a specific decision the team makes weekly.

Missing information before the dashboard can be designed:
- Who specifically is the audience? (team PM, leadership, cross-functional)
- What decisions does this dashboard need to support?
- What is the product type and primary engagement model?
- What metrics already exist and are being tracked?
