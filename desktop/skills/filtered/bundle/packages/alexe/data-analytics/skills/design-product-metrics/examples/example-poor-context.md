## Input
Help me define metrics for my product.

## Expected output
A metrics framework can't be designed without knowing what the product does or what it's trying to achieve.

What is known:
- There is a product that needs metrics

What must be confirmed before starting:
- What is the product? (type, business model, B2C or B2B)
- What is the main goal the metrics framework should serve? (activation, retention, revenue, engagement)
- Is there a specific product area or funnel in scope, or is this the full product?
- Are there existing metrics or OKRs that the framework should connect to?
- Is there any existing data infrastructure?

Suggested next inputs:
- Product description and business model
- The goal or OKR this metrics work is meant to support
- The product area in scope

Without these, any framework produced will be a generic template with no connection to real decisions.

Confidence level:
Provisional
