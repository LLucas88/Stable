## Input
Our planning is chaotic. We do quarterly planning but the roadmap is always wrong by month 2. We work in 2-week sprints. Team is about 10 people including engineers and PMs.

## Expected output
Planning levels (provisional):

Sprint (2-week):
- Exists already, presumably
- Sprint goals should map to quarterly priorities — if they don't, that's a signal the quarterly plan is disconnected from execution

Quarterly:
- Exists but producing plans that go stale by month 2
- This is a common failure: quarterly plans get made once and never updated. The fix is usually a structured mid-quarter review, not a better planning session.

Annual or strategic level:
- Not mentioned — unclear if this exists or if quarterly planning is disconnected from a longer horizon

Root cause assessment (provisional):
"Roadmap always wrong by month 2" could mean:
1. The quarterly plan was overcommitted from the start (capacity problem)
2. Priorities changed but the plan wasn't updated (change management problem)
3. Discovery during the quarter revealed the priorities were wrong (that's actually good — but needs a process to act on it)

Without knowing which of these applies, the planning redesign is hard to scope.

Cascade design:
Not designed — need to know whether there's a strategic layer above quarterly planning.

Key planning events:
At minimum: quarterly planning session + a mid-quarter review at week 6.

Strategy change protocol:
Not defined. This is likely the most important thing to add — if priorities can change mid-quarter without a documented process, the roadmap will always drift.

Documentation artifacts:
Not defined.

Missing information:
- Why does the roadmap go wrong? (over-commitment, changing priorities, or new discovery?)
- Is there an annual or strategic layer above quarterly planning?
- Who makes the call to change priorities mid-quarter?
