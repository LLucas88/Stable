## Input
We're a small product team — one PM, two engineers, one designer. We work in sprints but don't have a consistent cadence. We need to figure out what meetings and rituals to have.

## Expected output
Meeting cadence design (provisional):

Sprint review:
Frequency: end of each sprint (typically biweekly)
Duration: 30 minutes
Attendees: full team
Goal: review what shipped, check against sprint goals, make any course-correction decisions
Note: for a 4-person team, this can be informal but should still produce one explicit decision or next step documented

Retrospective:
Frequency: biweekly, after sprint review
Duration: 20-30 minutes
Goal: identify one thing to change next sprint — don't try to fix everything at once

Weekly sync (optional for this team size):
Frequency: weekly, 30 minutes
Attendees: all
Goal: surface blockers and dependency issues mid-sprint
Note: for a 4-person team, async standup updates may be enough — evaluate whether a weekly sync adds value

Planning session:
Frequency: beginning of each sprint
Duration: 1-2 hours
Goal: review backlog, agree on sprint scope, identify dependencies

Async norms:
Not yet defined — what tools are in use and what are the communication patterns?

Planning cycle design:
Not defined — what planning horizon do you work to? Quarterly OKRs? Ad-hoc roadmap? This affects the planning events needed beyond sprint-level.

Decision-making framework:
Not defined — for a 4-person team, this can be lightweight, but it's worth agreeing on: who has final say on product scope, who owns technical decisions.

Missing information:
- Sprint length
- Whether there are external stakeholders who need updates
- Planning horizon (quarterly, roadmap-driven, etc.)
- Current pain points — what's actually breaking?

Confidence level:
Provisional
