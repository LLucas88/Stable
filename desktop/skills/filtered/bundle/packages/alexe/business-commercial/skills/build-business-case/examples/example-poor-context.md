## Input
We need a business case for a new feature.

## Expected output
A business case requires knowing what's being built, what it costs, and what business outcome it's expected to produce.

What is known:
- A feature needs a business case

What must be confirmed:
- What is the feature? (scope and description)
- What business goal does it serve? (retention, conversion, expansion, cost reduction)
- What is the estimated development cost? (team size, timeline)
- What business outcome is expected? (quantitative or qualitative)

Without these, the output is a business case template with empty fields — not a business case.

Suggested next inputs:
- Feature description and scope
- Business goal being served
- Cost estimate (even rough)
- Expected outcome (even directional)

Confidence level:
Provisional
