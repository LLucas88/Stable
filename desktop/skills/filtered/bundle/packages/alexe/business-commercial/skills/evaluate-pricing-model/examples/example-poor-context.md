## Input
Should we change our pricing?

## Expected output
Pricing model evaluation requires knowing the current model, the business goals, and the customer context.

What is known:
- A pricing model exists that may need changing

What must be confirmed:
- What is the current pricing model? (per-seat, flat-fee, usage-based, freemium)
- What problem is the pricing expected to solve? (low growth, low NRR, competitive pressure, expansion friction)
- Who are the target customer segments?
- What is the current NRR, churn, or expansion data?

Without these, a pricing recommendation is untethered to the business reality.

Suggested next inputs:
- Current pricing model description
- Business goals (what outcome should the pricing model support)
- Data on NRR, churn, or pricing-related customer feedback

Confidence level:
Provisional
