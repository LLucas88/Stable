## Input
Is our roadmap aligned with revenue?

## Expected output
Revenue alignment can't be assessed without knowing the roadmap and the revenue model.

What is known:
- There is a roadmap that may or may not be revenue-aligned

What must be provided:
- What are the product initiatives on the roadmap? (descriptions, not just names)
- What is the revenue model? (subscription, transactional, usage-based, etc.)
- What are the commercial goals for the relevant period? (growth, churn reduction, NRR, expansion)

Without these, the question "is our roadmap aligned with revenue?" cannot be answered — and any answer would be speculation.

Suggested next inputs:
- Roadmap initiatives (even a bullet list)
- Revenue model description
- Commercial goals for the period

Confidence level:
Provisional
