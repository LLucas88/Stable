## Input
We're building a new feature that lets users export their data. It's on the roadmap for Q4. Revenue model is subscription, $20/month. Not sure if this helps the business commercially.

## Expected output
Initiative-to-revenue impact map:
Data export feature → revenue impact type: primarily indirect (retention / trust) rather than direct (conversion or expansion).

Data export is a feature users want available even if they rarely use it. Its value is in reducing the feeling of lock-in — users who know they can leave tend to stay longer. This is a retention play, not an acquisition or expansion play.

Commercial goal alignment assessment:
Without knowing the specific commercial goals (growth, churn reduction, NRR, new user acquisition), it's difficult to assess how much this aligns. However:
- If the commercial goal is reducing churn: data export may have a small positive effect by reducing "I can't get my data out" as a churn driver. This effect is likely modest unless data portability is a known complaint.
- If the commercial goal is acquisition or conversion: data export has little impact.
- If the business operates in a regulated space (GDPR-required) or serves B2B customers with procurement requirements: data export may be table stakes for closing deals — in which case it's a direct revenue enabler.

Conflict flags:
Data export is generally safe commercially. One edge case: if users can export everything and seamlessly migrate to a competitor, removing lock-in friction could increase switching. For most SaaS products this effect is small — users don't leave because they can't export; they leave because the product isn't working for them.

Commercial friction points:
If data export is being built because users are asking for it, understanding *why* they're asking is worth investigating. Are users asking because they want to migrate away? Or because they use the export for downstream workflows? These have very different commercial implications.

Alignment recommendations:
- If churn reduction is a commercial priority, a user exit survey or support ticket analysis would show whether data portability is in the top churn reasons. If it's not in the top 5, the feature has limited commercial ROI.
- If Q4 roadmap has more direct commercial drivers (retention features, expansion features), data export may be deprioritized without meaningful commercial impact.

Missing information:
- What are the current commercial goals for Q4?
- Is data export a regulatory requirement (GDPR, CCPA) or a product-driven addition?
- Are there customer requests or support tickets specifically citing lack of data export as a pain point?

Confidence level:
Context-informed
