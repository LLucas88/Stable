## Input
Business model: B2C subscription, mobile app, monthly plan only.
Monthly ARPU: $9.99
Gross margin: 71%
Monthly churn: 5.1%
Blended CAC: $24
CAC by channel: Google Ads $34, organic (App Store) $0, referral $9
Gross monthly MRR: $320,000
Monthly new customer acquisitions: ~800

## Expected output
Unit economics summary:

LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Monthly Churn
LTV = ($9.99 × 0.71) / 0.051 = $7.09 / 0.051 = approximately $139

CAC:
Blended: $24
Google Ads: $34
Organic: $0
Referral: $9

LTV:CAC ratio:
Blended: $139 / $24 = 5.8x
Google Ads: $139 / $34 = 4.1x
Organic: effectively infinite (no S&M cost)
Referral: $139 / $9 = 15.4x

Payback period (blended):
$24 / ($9.99 × 0.71) = $24 / $7.09 = 3.4 months

Benchmark comparison:
LTV:CAC 5.8x (blended): healthy — above the 3x threshold.
Payback 3.4 months: strong — well under the 12-month benchmark.
Google Ads at 4.1x: acceptable but with limited margin. Any CAC increase or churn rise would push this channel toward marginal.
Referral at 15.4x: excellent economics; referral users are significantly more efficient than paid.

Health assessment per metric:
- LTV $139: reasonable for a $9.99/month product at this churn rate. Sensitive to churn — a 1pp increase reduces LTV to $116 (16% decrease).
- Monthly churn 5.1%: elevated for B2C subscription. Average customer lifetime = 1/5.1% = ~20 months. This is the most critical improvement lever.
- Payback 3.4 months: strong. Even at higher churn scenarios, payback remains short due to low CAC.
- Blended CAC $24: competitive. Referral at $9 is the most efficient channel.

Key risk or gap identification:
Monthly churn at 5.1% is the dominant risk. If churn rises to 7%: LTV drops to $101, blended LTV:CAC drops to 4.2x (still healthy but tighter). If churn rises to 10%: LTV drops to $71, blended ratio drops to 2.9x (below the 3x threshold — marginal).

The business is healthy today but has limited buffer before churn becomes a structural problem. Retention is the highest-leverage metric.

Improvement levers:
1. Reduce monthly churn from 5.1% to 3.5%: LTV increases from $139 to $203 (+46%). Blended LTV:CAC improves from 5.8x to 8.5x. Impact: very high.
2. Grow referral share of new users: referral CAC is $9 vs. blended $24. Increasing referral from current share to 30% of new acquisitions reduces blended CAC materially without product changes.
3. Introduce annual plan option: annual subscribers typically churn at 40-60% lower monthly rate than monthly subscribers. Shifting 30% of the base to annual would likely reduce blended churn to ~3.5%.

Assumptions and confidence level:
- Churn rate: assumed stable month-over-month (constant churn model). If new cohorts churn higher than older cohorts (common early in growth), LTV may be overstated. Confidence: medium.
- Gross margin: assumed stable at 71%. Infrastructure costs may increase with scale. Confidence: medium.
- CAC by channel: assumes current channel mix is stable. Confidence: high for short-term planning.
