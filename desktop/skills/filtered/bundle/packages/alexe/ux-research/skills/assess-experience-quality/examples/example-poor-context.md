## Input
Our UX doesn't feel great. Can you assess it?

## Expected output
To assess experience quality, I need to know what to assess and what signals are available.

What is known:
- There is a concern about UX quality

What must be confirmed:
- Which product flow or feature to assess
- What "doesn't feel great" means — is this based on user feedback, metrics, internal opinion, or something else?
- What data exists: analytics, support tickets, user feedback, usability test results

Suggested approach:
1. Pick one specific flow or feature to start with (e.g., the flow with the highest drop-off, most support tickets, or most user complaints)
2. Gather available signals: funnel data, support tickets, qualitative feedback
3. Walk through the flow step by step and apply quality criteria: discoverability, clarity, efficiency, error recovery, trust
4. Identify friction points with evidence

Without a specific flow and supporting data, a quality assessment would be based entirely on heuristics, which is a starting point but not a reliable basis for design decisions.

- Which flow or feature to assess
- Any available data (analytics, support tickets, user feedback)
- What triggered the concern
