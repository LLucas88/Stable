## Input
We need to do some user research.

## Expected output
To plan user research, I need to understand what the research should answer.

What is known:
- The team wants to conduct user research

What must be confirmed:
- What decision will this research inform? (launch, redesign, feature priority, new market?)
- What product area is the focus?
- Who are the target users?
- What is already known? (existing data, prior research, assumptions)

Method depends on the goal:
- Understanding user needs or motivations → interviews
- Validating a design or flow → usability testing
- Measuring attitudes or preferences at scale → survey
- Understanding behavior in context → contextual inquiry or diary study
- Testing a concept before building → concept testing

Suggested next inputs:
- The question or decision this research should inform
- The product area involved
- The target user segment
- Timeline and budget constraints

Without a research question, any plan would be unfocused. Start with: "What do we need to learn, and what decision will it inform?"
