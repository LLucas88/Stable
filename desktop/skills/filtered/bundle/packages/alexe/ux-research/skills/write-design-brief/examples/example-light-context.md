## Input
Our clients struggle to give feedback on deliverables in our project management tool. We want the design team to look into improving this. Can you write a design brief?

## Expected output
Problem statement:
Clients have difficulty giving feedback on deliverables. The specific nature of the difficulty is not yet defined — it could be discoverability, workflow confusion, notification issues, or something else.

Target user and context:
Clients who review deliverables. Details needed: how often they review, what devices they use, how many projects they manage, their technical comfort level.

Desired outcome:
Clients can give feedback on deliverables more easily and reliably. Specific outcome metrics need to be defined.

Constraints:
Not provided — the following should be confirmed:
- Technical constraints (frontend framework, mobile requirements)
- Business constraints (which plans get this feature)
- Time constraints (design timeline, engineering capacity)
- Brand guidelines

Non-goals:
Not defined — recommend clarifying what this design effort should not address to prevent scope creep.

Success criteria (draft):
- To be defined. Consider: time to feedback, feedback completion rate, client satisfaction, reduction in follow-up requests from the agency side.

Supporting materials:
None provided. The following would strengthen this brief:
- Client feedback or interview data about the current experience
- Analytics: how many clients complete feedback, how long it takes
- Support tickets related to the feedback flow
- Screenshots or flow documentation of the current experience

Open questions for the designer to explore:
- What exactly do clients struggle with? (finding the feedback link, understanding what to comment on, knowing when they're done?)
- Where in the workflow does friction occur?
- What does the current feedback flow look like?

Missing information:
- What "struggle" means specifically (is this from user research, support tickets, or internal observation?)
- Target user profile
- Constraints (technical, time, business)
- Success criteria
- Non-goals

Suggested next steps:
1. Talk to 3–5 clients or review support tickets to understand what "struggle" means concretely
2. Document the current feedback flow
3. Define constraints and non-goals
4. Then finalize this brief before handing to design

Incomplete — this brief needs more context before it can guide design work effectively.
