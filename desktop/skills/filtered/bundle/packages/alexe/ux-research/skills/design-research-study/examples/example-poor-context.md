## Input
We need to do a research study.

## Expected output
To design a research study, I need to understand what question the study should answer.

What is known:
- The team wants to conduct research

What must be confirmed:
- What decision will this research inform?
- What product area or user behavior is the focus?
- Who are the target participants?
- What is already known (prior research, data, hypotheses)?

Study design depends on the research question:
- "Why do users do X?" → interviews or diary study
- "Can users complete this task?" → usability testing
- "Which version works better?" → A/B test or concept test
- "What do users need?" → contextual inquiry or interviews
- "How many users experience X?" → survey

Suggested next inputs:
- The research question or problem the team is trying to solve
- The product area involved
- Any existing data or hypotheses
- Timeline and resource constraints

Without a research question, any study design would be arbitrary.

Cannot design — no research question provided
