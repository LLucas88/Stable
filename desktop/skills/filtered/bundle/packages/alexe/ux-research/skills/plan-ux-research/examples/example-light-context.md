## Input
We're launching our fantasy sports platform in Europe and need to do user research. We want to understand European football fans and how they think about fantasy sports.

## Expected output
Research question(s) (provisional):
1. How do European football fans currently engage with fantasy sports or similar games?
2. What would motivate them to try a new fantasy football platform?

Selected method: Semi-structured interviews (recommended)

Rationale: Exploring mental models and motivations in an unfamiliar market requires open-ended conversation. Surveys would be premature without knowing what to ask about.

Participant criteria (draft):
- European football fans (active — watch matches, follow a club)
- Mix of fantasy sports experience levels (current players, former, never tried)
- Target: 8–10 participants
- Markets: not specified — which European countries are you launching in first?

Discussion guide outline (draft):
1. Sports fandom: how they follow football, social context
2. Fantasy sports experience: have they played, what did they think
3. Mental models: what they expect from a fantasy football app
4. Motivations and barriers: what would make them try or avoid it

Missing information:
- Which European markets specifically (UK, Germany, France, Spain?)
- Timeline and budget
- Whether US user research exists that could inform the European study
- Whether a designer or researcher will moderate or if the PM will run sessions
- Language considerations — will sessions be in English or local languages?
- How many participants and what incentive

Suggested next steps:
1. Define target launch markets
2. Confirm budget, timeline, and who moderates
3. Decide on language approach (English-only or translated)
4. Then finalize participant criteria and discussion guide

Confidence level:
Provisional — method is appropriate but logistics and scope need to be confirmed.
