## Input
How do I hand off a design?

## Expected output
A design handoff is the process of transferring a completed design to the team that will build it, with enough context for them to build it correctly.

To structure a handoff, I need to understand the situation:

What must be confirmed:
- What feature or flow is being handed off?
- Who is handing off to whom? (product → design, or design → engineering?)
- What artifacts exist today? (Figma file, requirements doc, prototype)
- What team is receiving the handoff? (size, familiarity with the product)
- Has the team done handoffs before, and what went wrong?

A good handoff typically includes:
1. Readiness criteria: what must be complete before handoff (annotations, edge cases, specs)
2. Artifacts: the design file, interaction specs, component mapping, copy
3. Review process: a walkthrough session + time for the receiving team to ask questions
4. Feedback protocol: how questions and issues flow back during build
5. Sign-off: who confirms the handoff is complete

Suggested next inputs:
- The feature being handed off
- Current state of design artifacts
- Teams involved
- Any known problems with past handoffs

Cannot structure — no feature or team context provided
