# Design Team Rituals

## Purpose
Help teams design rituals and recurring practices that build psychological safety, operating discipline, and shared context.

## Skill type
Conceptual skill

## Use this skill when
- A team lacks rituals that build shared context and alignment
- Existing team meetings feel wasteful or misaligned with team needs
- A new team is establishing its working culture and practices
- A team retrospective or reset is happening and rituals need to be redesigned

## Do not use this skill when
- The goal is operating cadence for delivery and planning (use design-operating-cadence)
- The goal is cross-team alignment systems (use align-cross-team-communication)

## Required inputs
- Team type and composition
- Current state of team rituals (even if "none")
- Key team goals or challenges

## Optional inputs
- Team size
- Remote vs. in-person vs. hybrid setup
- Current pain points in team dynamics
- Desired culture or working norms

## Upstream context
Works best when:
- Team goals and strategy are defined
- Operating cadence is designed

## Downstream handoff
Output can feed:
- align-cross-team-communication (team rituals feed into cross-team alignment)

## Instructions
1. Identify the team's core needs: alignment, learning, feedback, celebration, decision-making.
2. Map existing rituals and assess their value.
3. Design or refine rituals for each need.
4. Define ritual structure: cadence, duration, format, facilitation, output.
5. Identify who owns ritual design and evolution.
6. Define how the team will evaluate and adjust rituals over time.

## Output
Provide:
- Team needs assessment
- Current ritual audit
- Recommended ritual set with: cadence, duration, format, facilitation, purpose
- Ritual ownership and maintenance plan
- Ritual effectiveness review process
- What to stop, start, and continue

## Risks / caveats
- Rituals imposed from outside rarely stick — co-create with the team
- Too many rituals crowd out actual work — be selective
- Rituals need regular review — what works at team size 5 fails at 20
