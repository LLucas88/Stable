## Input
Help me with stakeholder management.

## Expected output
Stakeholder mapping requires knowing the initiative and who is involved.

What is needed:
- The product initiative or decision requiring stakeholder engagement: what are you working on?
- A list of stakeholders involved (even rough): who are the key people or teams?

Without these inputs, it's not possible to assess influence, interest, or engagement strategy — those depend entirely on the specific context.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The initiative is [description of what you're working on or deciding]"
- "The stakeholders I'm aware of are [names or teams]"
- Optional: "The main challenge is [why stakeholder management is difficult here]"
