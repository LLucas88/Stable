## Input
Help me make a decision.

## Expected output
A decision framework requires knowing what decision needs to be made and who is involved.

What is needed:
- The decision: what specifically needs to be decided?
- Relevant stakeholders: who is involved in or affected by this decision?

Without these inputs, there is nothing to structure.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The decision is: [what needs to be decided]"
- "The stakeholders are: [who is involved]"
- Optional: "We've been stuck because [reason the decision is stalled]"
