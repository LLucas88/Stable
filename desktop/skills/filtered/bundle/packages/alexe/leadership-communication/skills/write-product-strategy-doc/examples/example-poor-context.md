## Input
Write a product strategy doc for us.

## Expected output
A product strategy document requires knowing the product area and the strategic direction before it can be written.

What is needed:
- Product area or scope: what is this strategy for?
- Strategic direction and key decisions to document: what direction is being set and what choices are being made?

Without these inputs, any strategy document produced would be a template, not a strategy.

Strategy documents that describe everything are strategy documents about nothing. A real strategy doc makes choices — it says what you're doing and what you're explicitly not doing.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The product area is [description]"
- "The strategic direction we've decided is [direction]"
- "The key choice we're making is [option chosen over another option]"
- "The 12-month success definition is [specific outcome]"
