## Input
We have a small product team and we want to set up good team rituals. We currently have standup and nothing else. The team is remote.

## Expected output
Team needs assessment (provisional — based on what's provided):
Remote teams without rituals beyond standup typically lack: structured alignment on direction, space for feedback and learning, and moments for relationship-building that don't happen in a distributed environment.

---

Current ritual audit:
- Daily standup: exists. Format and quality unknown. "Standup" can mean anything from a useful 10-minute sync to a 45-minute status update that wastes everyone's time.

Before adding rituals, it's worth asking: is the current standup working? If it's already too much meeting, adding more rituals won't help. If it's too shallow, the new rituals should go deeper, not wider.

---

Recommended starting set (minimal — 3 rituals for a small remote team):

**1. Weekly team sync (45–60 min)**
Purpose: real alignment and decisions — not status updates (standup handles that)
Format: sprint progress, current work-in-progress review, decisions that need the team
Cadence: weekly, same time every week
Who: full team

**2. Bi-weekly or monthly retro (45–60 min)**
Purpose: honest reflection and process improvement
Format: What went well / What didn't / What to change
Cadence: every 2 weeks (if you're doing sprints) or monthly otherwise
Note: this is the ritual most teams skip and most teams need

**3. Async weekly update (5 min per person)**
Purpose: shared context without a meeting
Format: each person posts a 3-bullet update on Friday (what I finished, what I'm starting Monday, one thing I need)
Why: remote teams lose context between meetings; async updates keep everyone informed without adding meeting time

---

Missing context before finalizing:
- Team size and composition (roles)? (This changes which rituals are needed and how long they should run)
- What are the current pain points? (Alignment? Decision-making? Feedback? Trust?) These determine which ritual should be prioritized
- What is the team working rhythm? (2-week sprints? Continuous? Kanban?) Rituals should sync with the delivery cadence

---

Recommendation: don't add all three at once. Start with the weekly sync, run for 3 weeks, then add the retro. Over-ritualizing a small team is as bad as under-ritualizing it.
