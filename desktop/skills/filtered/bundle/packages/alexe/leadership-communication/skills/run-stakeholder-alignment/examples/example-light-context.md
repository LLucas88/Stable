## Input
We need to align stakeholders on our Q3 roadmap. Some people have concerns but I'm not sure exactly who is skeptical or why. We have a planning meeting in 10 days.

## Expected output
Alignment goal:
Reach shared agreement on the Q3 roadmap before or during the planning meeting in 10 days.

---

Must-align stakeholders (to be identified):

The first step is knowing who must align vs. who just needs to be informed. Before the planning meeting, identify:
- Who can block the roadmap if they don't align? (Typically: engineering leadership for capacity decisions, finance for budget-dependent items, leadership for strategic direction changes)
- Who has concerns but no blocking authority? (They need to be heard, not necessarily to agree)
- Who just needs to be informed? (Share the output, don't spend alignment time on them)

Without knowing who the skeptics are and what they're skeptical about, you can't prepare for the right conversations.

---

Recommended steps before the planning meeting:

**Step 1: Map the skeptics (Days 1–3)**
Reach out informally to the 2–3 people you most expect to have concerns. 10-minute conversations: "I'm preparing for the planning session — are there any directions on the Q3 roadmap you'd want to understand better or push back on?" This surfaces concerns before the room, not in the room.

**Step 2: Prepare for specific objections (Days 4–6)**
Once you know the concerns, prepare responses:
- If the concern is capacity/timeline: have the engineering capacity estimate ready
- If the concern is strategic priority: have the OKR-to-roadmap mapping ready
- If the concern is a specific initiative: know your rationale and the trade-off you made

**Step 3: Pre-align high-influence skeptics before the meeting (Days 7–9)**
A planning meeting is a bad place to resolve fundamental disagreements for the first time. If a stakeholder is going to push back hard, have the conversation 1:1 before the group session.

---

Success definition for this alignment:
At the end of the planning meeting: each must-align stakeholder can restate the roadmap direction in their own words (not just nod). Any explicit concerns are documented along with the rationale for the decision made.

---

Missing context that would sharpen this plan:
- Who are the specific stakeholders involved?
- What are the specific items on the Q3 roadmap that are most likely to generate disagreement?
- Have there been previous roadmap disagreements that might repeat here?
