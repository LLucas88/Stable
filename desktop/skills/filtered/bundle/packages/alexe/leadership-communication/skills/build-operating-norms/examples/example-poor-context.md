## Input
Write team norms for us.

## Expected output
Team norms require two inputs before they can be written: the team's context (size, structure, friction points) and the team's participation in creating them.

What is needed:
- Team type, size, and structure: who is on this team?
- Known friction points or gaps: what specific working problems are the norms trying to solve?

Without knowing the friction points, the norms will be generic and won't address the actual problems.

Important: norms written by one person for others are rules, not agreements. The output here is a draft to discuss with the team — not a final document to hand down.

Suggested next inputs:
- "Our team is [size and roles]"
- "The friction we're experiencing is [specific problems: decisions, communication, availability, feedback, etc.]"
- "We're [remote / hybrid / in-person]"
