## Input
I need to align on a decision to cut our free tier. We currently have a free plan (limited features) and a paid plan ($12/month). My recommendation is to eliminate the free tier entirely and convert free users to a 14-day trial instead.

The business case: our free tier users have a 1.2% conversion to paid rate and require the same infrastructure cost as paid users. The 14-day trial has shown (from a 3-month pilot on new users) a 6.8% conversion rate.

Current stakeholder stances:
- CEO: tentatively aligned but wants to see the analysis documented
- Head of Growth: skeptical — believes free tier drives word-of-mouth and long-term brand equity; hasn't seen the trial conversion data
- Head of CS: neutral; concerned about the free user communication and support spike during migration
- CFO: supportive — this is a cost reduction and conversion improvement story
- VP Engineering: neutral; needs scope for migration work
- Board: not involved yet; CEO wants to align internally before board presentation

Decision timeline: need to decide within 2 weeks to hit Q3 planning.

## Expected output
Alignment goal:
Reach a decision to proceed with eliminating the free tier (or an explicit decision not to) by end of week 2, with full internal team alignment before board presentation.

---

Must-align stakeholders with stances:

| Stakeholder | Current stance | Key concern |
|------------|---------------|------------|
| CEO | Tentatively aligned | Wants documented analysis before committing |
| Head of Growth | Skeptic | Believes free tier is a brand/word-of-mouth asset; hasn't seen trial data |
| Head of CS | Neutral | Migration communication and support spike risk |
| CFO | Supporter | None identified |
| VP Engineering | Neutral | Scope of migration work |

Note: CFO and VP Engineering are aligned or neutral — they're not blockers. The alignment work is concentrated on CEO and Head of Growth.

---

Engagement sequence:

**Step 1: Document the analysis (this week, Day 1–2)**
Before any stakeholder conversations, write the 1-page decision brief:
- Current free tier economics (conversion rate, cost, infrastructure)
- Trial pilot results (6.8% conversion, cohort source, duration)
- Migration plan (timeline, user communication approach)
- What "success" looks like at 6 months
Share with CEO first, then Head of Growth.

**Step 2: 1:1 with Head of Growth (Day 3)**
This is the critical alignment conversation.
Their concern: free tier = word-of-mouth and brand equity.
Your response: the pilot data shows trial users convert at 5.6x the rate of free users. If the word-of-mouth value of free users is real, it would need to add approximately [calculate: what acquisition volume from word-of-mouth would offset the conversion loss].
What you're willing to adjust: if Head of Growth has specific evidence (referral data, attribution data showing free-user word-of-mouth) that would change the economics, you want to see it before the decision is finalized.
What is not negotiable: continuing a free tier with 1.2% conversion when the trial has demonstrated 6.8% is not a sustainable model.

**Step 3: 1:1 with CEO (Day 4)**
Goal: confirm alignment after sharing the written analysis. Address any remaining questions.
Share the brief + summary of the Head of Growth conversation. If Growth has raised new evidence, incorporate it or explain why it doesn't change the recommendation.

**Step 4: CS alignment on migration plan (Day 4–5)**
CS's concern is practical, not strategic. Meet with Head of CS to define: user communication timeline, support team prep, expected spike volume, and how many weeks of heads-up free users will get. CS needs specifics to feel comfortable, not a general "we'll handle it."

**Step 5: VP Engineering scope (Day 5)**
30-minute conversation: walk through the technical scope for free tier removal, timeline implications for Q3, and confirm there are no engineering blockers that would change the decision.

**Step 6: Full team sync (Day 7–8)**
Once CEO, Growth, and CS are aligned (or alignment gap is explicit), bring the full team together. Present the analysis, the stakeholder input incorporated, the decision, and the next steps. This is a ratification meeting, not a debate meeting.

---

Preparation per key stakeholder:

**Head of Growth:**
Concerns: brand equity, word-of-mouth, long-term user acquisition
My response: acknowledge the concern as legitimate. Ask if there is data to support it — if yes, incorporate it. If not, the 6.8% vs. 1.2% data makes the risk calculable and acceptable.
Negotiable: I am willing to consider a hybrid approach (no free tier for new users, grandfather existing free users for 6 months) if Growth provides data on free-user referral volume. This doesn't change the direction — it changes the migration approach.
Not negotiable: I will not delay the decision for a philosophical disagreement about brand equity without data.

**Head of CS:**
Concerns: support volume spike, user communication
My response: CS is right to flag this. I will build a migration communication plan (email cadence, FAQ, support scripts) with their input before the decision is communicated publicly. Their concern is not a reason to not make the decision; it is a reason to do it carefully.

---

Success definition for alignment:
Not "no objections" — but:
- Head of Growth either presents data that changes the recommendation or explicitly accepts the decision with documented concerns
- CEO approves the written analysis and confirms authority to proceed
- CS has a migration communication plan they are comfortable with
- VP Engineering has confirmed Q3 engineering capacity

---

Follow-up plan to maintain alignment:
- After decision is made: distribute written decision record to all stakeholders (including those who objected) — what was decided, the rationale, their concern, and why the recommendation held
- At 30 days post-launch: review conversion rate of the new trial cohort vs. the 6.8% benchmark. Share with Head of Growth specifically — this closes the loop on their concern with real data.

---

Contingency if alignment isn't reached:
If Head of Growth presents strong word-of-mouth data (referral data, attribution analysis) that materially changes the cost-benefit, the decision should be reopened with new data, not pushed through.
If alignment cannot be reached within 2 weeks, escalate to CEO for a final decision — the decision cannot wait indefinitely for consensus.
