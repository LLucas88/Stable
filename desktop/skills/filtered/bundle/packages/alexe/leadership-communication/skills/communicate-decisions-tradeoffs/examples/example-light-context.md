## Input
We've decided to deprioritize the enterprise tier we were planning to build. We need to communicate this to the sales team and some early enterprise customers we had conversations with.

## Expected output
Decision statement:
The enterprise tier development has been deprioritized. There is no launch date for enterprise features at this time.

---

Alternatives considered (to fill in):
Before communicating this, it helps to name what was evaluated. Was this a pure resource decision? Did market signals change? Was the enterprise tier expected to solve a problem that turned out to have a different solution? Being specific about what was weighed makes the decision more credible.

---

Trade-off summary (provisional — needs your inputs):

What was chosen: focus on [existing segment or strategy] rather than building enterprise tier
What is being given up: enterprise sales motion, potential revenue from enterprise accounts in pipeline, credibility with early enterprise contacts who expected this to move forward

---

Objection handling (likely concerns to address):

*From the sales team:*
"We had enterprise deals in the pipeline. What do we tell them?"
Address this directly: give sales a specific script or talking points. "We're unable to support enterprise at our current scale" is more honest than "we're still evaluating timing." Give them a clear answer they can use.

*From early enterprise contacts:*
"You said you were building this."
Acknowledge the expectation directly. Don't send a generic update. Each contact who was given a specific commitment should receive a personal message — not a bulk email.

---

Missing context before finalizing communication:
- Why was it deprioritized? (Resources, market signal, strategic shift) — this changes the communication completely
- Were any enterprise customers given a specific timeline or commitment? (If yes, they need personal outreach, not a mass communication)
- Is this permanent ("we're not building enterprise") or indefinite ("not now")? Be honest — "maybe later" that will never happen is worse than "not on our roadmap"

---

Communication format recommendation:
- Sales team: briefing call + FAQ document (before they hear from customers)
- Enterprise contacts with prior commitments: personal email or call from PM or CEO
- General communication: update any public materials that implied enterprise tier was coming
