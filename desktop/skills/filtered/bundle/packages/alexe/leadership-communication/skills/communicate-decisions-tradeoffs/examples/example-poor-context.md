## Input
Help me communicate a decision.

## Expected output
Decision communication requires knowing what was decided and who is affected.

What is needed:
- The decision made: what specifically was decided?
- Affected stakeholders or teams: who needs to hear about this?

Without these inputs, there is no decision to communicate.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The decision is: [what was decided]"
- "The affected stakeholders are: [who is impacted or needs to know]"
- Optional but useful: "The rationale for the decision is [reasoning]" and "Known concerns are [objections you expect]"
