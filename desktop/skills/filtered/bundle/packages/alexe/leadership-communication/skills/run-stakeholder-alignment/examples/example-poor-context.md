## Input
Help me align stakeholders.

## Expected output
Stakeholder alignment requires knowing what decision or initiative needs alignment and who the stakeholders are.

What is needed:
- Decision or initiative requiring alignment: what specifically needs to be agreed on?
- Stakeholders involved and their current stances: who needs to align, and where do they stand now?

Without these inputs, any alignment plan would be a generic process template rather than targeted preparation for the specific situation.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The decision is: [what needs to be agreed on]"
- "The stakeholders are [names or roles] and their stances are [supportive / neutral / skeptical]"
- Optional: "Prior alignment attempts: [what has been tried and what went wrong]"
