# 🎤 Leadership, Communication & Stakeholder Management

[← Back to all domains](../README.md)

Skills for building executive narratives, communicating decisions, mapping stakeholders, applying decision frameworks, and designing team operating norms.

Product work is 50% building the right thing and 50% getting people aligned to build it. This domain gives your agent the frameworks for the second half: how to make decisions transparently, communicate trade-offs honestly, navigate stakeholder politics, and establish the team norms that make everything else work.

- `build-executive-narrative` structures presentations for leadership → feeds `communicate-decisions-tradeoffs`

**Decision-making:**

- `map-stakeholder-influence` identifies influencers and their concerns → feeds `run-stakeholder-alignment`

## 🛠️ Skills in this domain

### 1. `build-executive-narrative`

Use this when a board or leadership presentation needs structuring, or the team has data but no clear story.

What it does:
- Identifies the one thing the audience should leave knowing
- Builds the arc: context → insight → decision/direction → ask
- Cuts ruthlessly — executives want clarity, not comprehensiveness

---

### 2. `write-product-strategy-doc`

Use this when the team lacks a written strategy and relies on oral tradition, or strategic debates keep recurring.

What it does:
- Documents direction clearly enough to align teams and guide prioritization
- Requires explicit trade-offs — "we will do X and Y and Z" is not strategy
- Assigns an owner so the document stays current

---

### 3. `apply-decision-framework`

Use this when a decision is stalled because authority is unclear, or a contested decision needs a structured process.

What it does:
- Selects the right framework (DACI, SPADE, RAPID)
- Clarifies: who decides, who inputs, who is informed
- Documents the decision before communicating it

---

### 4. `communicate-decisions-tradeoffs`

Use this when a difficult decision needs to be shared with stakeholders, or a deprioritization requires explanation.

What it does:
- States the decision clearly and early
- Explains what was considered: alternatives, trade-offs, constraints
- Acknowledges trade-offs honestly — builds more credibility than pretending it's perfect

---

### 5. `map-stakeholder-influence`

Use this when an initiative requires buy-in from multiple stakeholders, or dynamics are creating roadblocks.

What it does:
- Maps all stakeholders by interest and influence
- Identifies informal influence, not just org chart authority
- Develops targeted engagement strategies per stakeholder

---

### 6. `run-stakeholder-alignment`

Use this when stakeholders disagree on direction, or alignment keeps breaking down in execution.

What it does:
- Defines what specific agreement must be reached
- Designs the alignment process (not just a meeting)
- Tests for real alignment — "no objections" is not alignment

---

### 7. `build-operating-norms`

Use this when a new team is forming, or recurring friction indicates norms aren't working.

What it does:
- Establishes explicit norms: communication, decision-making, feedback, conflict resolution
- Co-creates with the team (norms written by one person are rules)
- Keeps the list short — too many norms create bureaucracy

---

### 8. `design-team-rituals`

Use this when the team lacks practices that build shared context, or existing meetings feel wasteful.

What it does:
- Identifies core team needs: alignment, learning, feedback, celebration
- Maps existing rituals and assesses their value
- Designs new rituals for team size and maturity

## 🧭 How to use this domain

### Typical paths:

**Presenting to leadership:**
1. `write-product-strategy-doc` → anchor document
2. `build-executive-narrative` → presentation

**Making a contested decision:**
1. `map-stakeholder-influence` → understand the landscape
2. `apply-decision-framework` → structure the decision
3. `run-stakeholder-alignment` → build real alignment
4. `communicate-decisions-tradeoffs` → share the outcome

**Building team culture:**
1. `build-operating-norms` → establish how the team works
2. `design-team-rituals` → create practices that reinforce norms

**Enter in the middle:**
- Decision stalled → start with `apply-decision-framework`
- Stakeholders blocking → start with `map-stakeholder-influence`
- Need a board presentation → start with `build-executive-narrative`
- Team friction → start with `build-operating-norms`

## ⚠️ What this domain protects against

- **Decisions without rationale.** Decisions communicated without "why" generate distrust. `communicate-decisions-tradeoffs` always includes trade-off reasoning.
- **Committee decisions.** Too many approvers produce weak outcomes. `apply-decision-framework` ensures one clear decision-maker.
- **Surprising stakeholders.** Stakeholders surprised at the last moment become active opponents. `map-stakeholder-influence` + `run-stakeholder-alignment` engage them early.
- **Strategy as oral tradition.** Undocumented strategy gets reinterpreted. `write-product-strategy-doc` creates the anchor.
- **Norms imposed from above.** Norms written by one person for others are rules, not agreements. `build-operating-norms` co-creates with the team.
