## Input
We have about 200 support tickets from last month and some App Store reviews. Most tickets are about the same few things — onboarding confusion and a feature people keep asking for. I want to turn this into themes for the team.

## Expected output
Feedback source inventory:
- Support tickets: ~200 (last month)
- App Store reviews: volume not specified

Synthesis scope: limited — two sources, one month. Useful as a starting point but not a complete picture of customer voice.

---

Theme set (provisional — based on your summary):

**Theme 1: Onboarding confusion**
Volume: present in support tickets (exact % unknown)
Description: Users are confused during onboarding — specific confusion points not yet identified from your description
Representative signals: support tickets — exact quotes needed to characterize this
Implicit need: either the steps are unclear, the value proposition isn't landing early enough, or users are reaching onboarding without the right expectations
Recommended action: Read 10–15 onboarding-related tickets specifically. Look for the question or step where confusion occurs. The location of confusion matters — confusion at step 1 is different from confusion at step 5.

**Theme 2: Feature request (to be named)**
Volume: present in support tickets (exact % unknown)
Description: A feature people "keep asking for" — not yet identified
Recommended action: Before treating this as confirmed demand, check: are these requests from the same 5 users or from 50 different users? Recurring requests from a small vocal group look like a pattern but may not be.

---

What would make this synthesis more complete:
- Ticket tagging: how are the 200 tickets distributed across the two themes? If 90% are about onboarding, that's a different priority than 40%/40%/20%.
- App Store reviews: what volume and rating trend? Reviews can signal the emotional intensity of the themes.
- Segment breakdown: are onboarding complaints coming from all users or a specific plan, acquisition source, or geography?
- Customer type context: who are the users complaining? New users, long-term, specific plan tiers?

Minimum output for the team based on what's available:
- Theme 1: Onboarding confusion — [X]% of tickets, affects [segment if known], specific pain point is [step or content if identified]
- Theme 2: Feature request — [X]% of tickets, requested by [segment if known], specifically asking for [feature]

Fill in the brackets and this is a serviceable 15-minute synthesis for a planning meeting.
