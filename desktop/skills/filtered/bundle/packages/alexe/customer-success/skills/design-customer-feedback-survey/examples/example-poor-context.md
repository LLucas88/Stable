## Input
Write me a customer survey.

## Expected output
A survey cannot be designed without knowing what decision it's meant to inform.

What is needed:
- Survey goal: what question are you trying to answer? What decision will the results inform?
- Target respondents: who should fill out this survey?

Without these inputs, any survey written would collect data that may not be usable for any specific decision.

What must be confirmed:
- What is the one thing you most need to know from customers right now?
- Who are you asking — all users, a specific segment, churned customers?
- What will you do with the results? (This shapes how questions should be framed)

Suggested next inputs:
- "We want to understand [specific question] so we can [decision or action]"
- "The audience is [segment description]"
