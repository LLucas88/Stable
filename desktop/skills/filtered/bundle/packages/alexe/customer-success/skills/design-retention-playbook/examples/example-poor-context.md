## Input
Build a retention playbook for us.

## Expected output
A retention playbook requires churn root causes before it can be designed. Without knowing why customers leave, any "playbook" produced would be a list of generic tactics, not a targeted intervention plan.

What is needed:
- Churn root causes (from analysis, exit surveys, or known patterns): why are customers leaving?
- Customer segments: who is churning? (New customers, long-term, specific plan tiers)
- CS team structure and capacity: who will execute these plays, and how much time do they have?

Without churn root causes, it's not possible to match the right intervention to the right signal.

If churn root causes are unknown, the first step is churn analysis (use analyze-churn-retention), not playbook design.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The main reasons customers leave are [list from exit surveys or analysis]"
- "Our customer segments are [description]"
- "Our CS team is [size and structure]"
