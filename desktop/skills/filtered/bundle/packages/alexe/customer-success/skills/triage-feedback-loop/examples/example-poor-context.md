## Input
How do I handle customer feedback?

## Expected output
A feedback triage system requires knowing your current feedback sources before a process can be designed.

What is needed:
- Current feedback sources: where does feedback arrive? (Support chat, email, reviews, surveys, feature request boards, calls)
- Teams involved: who currently receives feedback and who needs to act on it?

Without knowing where feedback comes from and who is involved, any process designed would be generic and may not match your actual flow.

Suggested next inputs:
- "Our feedback comes in through [sources]"
- "Currently [CS team / PM / founder] handles it, but [problem: it doesn't reach product / it gets lost / there's too much to review]"
- "Our rough volume is [X tickets / messages per month]"
