## Input
How do I run a VoC program?

## Expected output
A VoC program design requires context about your current state and your product before it can be structured.

What is needed:
- Product type and customer segment: who are your customers and what do they use the product for?
- Current state of feedback collection: what channels do you have now, even if informal? (Support tickets, reviews, interviews, NPS, etc.)

Without knowing what already exists, the program design may duplicate things you already have or recommend channels that don't fit your customer type.

Suggested next inputs:
- "Our product is [description] and our customers are [segment]"
- "Currently we collect feedback through [channels] but it's [problem: not systematic / not reaching product / too much noise / etc.]"
- "The main thing we want to understand from customers is [question or decision]"
