## Input
We want to run a Voice of Customer program. Right now we're just reading support tickets sometimes. We have a SaaS product and we want to be more systematic.

## Expected output
Feedback channel audit:

Current state: support tickets reviewed occasionally. No other formal feedback channels confirmed.

Key gap: support tickets are primarily reactive — they capture problems customers chose to report. They miss silent dissatisfaction (users who stop using the product without filing a ticket) and don't capture why customers stay or what they value most.

---

Minimum viable VoC program (starting point):

**Tier 1: Improve what you already have**
- Support tickets: add consistent tagging before doing anything else. Tags: product area + issue type (bug / feature request / question / complaint). This turns a noise source into a signal source.
- Estimated effort: 2 hours to define taxonomy + ongoing tagging

**Tier 2: Add a feedback touchpoint**
- In-app or email NPS survey, quarterly. 2–3 questions maximum. This gives you a baseline sentiment score you can track over time.
- Estimated effort: 1 day to set up in a standard tool (Typeform, Intercom, etc.)

**Tier 3: Add qualitative depth (monthly)**
- 2 customer interviews per month (30 min each). Rotate between satisfied and at-risk customers.
- Estimated effort: 2 hours/month (PM time) + customer scheduling

---

Review cadence (starting point):
- Monthly: review support tags + pull top 5 themes (30 min)
- Quarterly: NPS synthesis + interview themes reviewed at planning meeting

---

Routing protocol (minimal):
- A theme appearing in 3+ support tickets this month: add to backlog consideration
- A pattern appearing in 2+ customer interviews: raise at next product review

---

What's missing before this becomes a full program:
- Product goals: what decisions should this VoC program inform? Without a connection to product decisions, the data will pile up unused.
- Team capacity: who owns this, how many hours per week?
- Customer segments: do different customer types have different needs? Segment data will determine whether aggregate feedback is misleading.
