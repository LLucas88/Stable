## Input

Product: Habito — a B2C mobile app for habit tracking and daily routines.

Context: "From our research, we know users struggle to maintain streaks beyond two weeks. We think there's an opportunity around better streak recovery, but we haven't formally validated it."

## Expected output

Opportunity 1 (provisional):
- HMW statement: How might we help users recover from a broken streak without losing motivation, so that a single missed day doesn't end a habit-building attempt?
- Source insight: Users struggle to maintain streaks beyond two weeks. The specific mechanism of disengagement after a streak break is not yet confirmed.
- Affected segment: Provisional — likely active users who have built a streak of 7+ days, but segment not confirmed from research.
- Potential impact: Medium (assumed) — directional potential is plausible, but the magnitude depends on how many users break streaks vs. never start them, and on whether streak breaks are the actual cause of churn or a symptom of something else.
- Evidence quality: Assumed — "struggling to maintain streaks" is a broad observation. Whether streak mechanics are the problem or whether the underlying habits are poorly chosen is not yet established.
- Risks: This opportunity statement assumes streaks are the right engagement mechanism. If users find streaks stressful rather than motivating, improving streak recovery may not address the real problem.
- Suggested next step: Narrow the insight first. Re-analyse existing research to understand what happens emotionally and behaviourally when a streak breaks. Is it disappointment, relief, or indifference? The answer shapes whether recovery, forgiveness, or a different mechanism is the right direction.

Opportunity 2 (provisional):
- HMW statement: How might we make the first two weeks of habit building feel rewarding enough that users reach the point where the habit feels automatic?
- Source insight: Team believes the two-week mark is critical. Not yet validated whether this is a product problem or a general habit formation challenge.
- Affected segment: New users (assumed).
- Potential impact: Unknown — depends on whether the two-week drop is specific to Habito's design or inherent to habit formation psychology.
- Evidence quality: Assumed — based on team observation of streak data, not user research into the cause.
- Risks: Investing in early-stage engagement features before understanding why users drop at two weeks risks building the wrong thing.
- Suggested next step: Validate the two-week drop pattern in cohort data. If confirmed, run 5–6 interviews with users who dropped at the two-week mark and users who pushed through it to understand the difference.

Note: Both opportunities are provisional and not ready for roadmap prioritisation or stakeholder presentation without additional research or data validation.
