## Input

"Synthesise our research."

## Expected output

Cannot synthesise without research data to work from.

Producing placeholder themes or generic findings without data would create the appearance of insight while providing none — and risks those placeholder themes being treated as real findings in downstream decisions.

To proceed with synthesis, provide at least one of the following:
- Interview notes or transcripts (even rough)
- Quotes or paraphrased observations from user conversations
- Support ticket summaries or categorised themes
- Survey responses or NPS comment exports
- JTBD mapping outputs

Also useful but not required:
- The research questions the data was collected to answer
- The user segment studied
- Any prior assumptions the team held before the research

Without data, synthesis is not possible. The recommended next step is to return with research material — then this skill can structure it into themes, insights, and gaps.
