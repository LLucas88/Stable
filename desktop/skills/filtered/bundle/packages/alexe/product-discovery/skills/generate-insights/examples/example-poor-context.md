## Input

"Can you generate some insights for us?"

## Expected output

Insights must come from research data — they cannot be generated without observations to interpret.

Producing generic insights without data would create plausible-sounding statements that carry no evidential weight. The risk is that those statements get treated as findings and drive product decisions that are not grounded in user reality.

To generate insights, provide research material such as:
- Interview notes or quotes
- Synthesised themes from qualitative research
- Support ticket summaries
- NPS or survey comment exports
- Behavioural observation notes

Also helpful: the research questions being answered and the user segment studied.

Without data, this skill cannot proceed. Return to run-user-interviews or synthesize-qualitative-research first, then come back with research material to turn into insights.
