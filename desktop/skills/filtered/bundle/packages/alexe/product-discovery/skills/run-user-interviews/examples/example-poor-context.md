## Input

"Can you give me some interview questions?"

## Expected output

Cannot produce a reliable interview guide without a research goal.

Generic interview questions produce generic answers — which tend to confirm what the team already believes rather than surface genuine insight. This is the most common failure mode of user research.

Questions to define before designing a guide:
1. What are you trying to learn from these interviews?
2. Who will you be interviewing — which users, at what stage in the product, with what behaviour?
3. What decision will the findings inform?

Once those three questions are answered, a guide with warm-up, context-setting, core questions, and wrap-up can be produced that is designed to generate real insight rather than confirmation.

If the goal is not yet defined, start with scope-discovery-research to establish what the research must answer before designing how to run it.
