## Input

Product: Booksy — a B2C audiobook subscription app.

Context: "We want to interview users about why they cancel their subscription. We haven't decided how many interviews or exactly what we want to learn."

## Expected output

Research goal (provisional): Understand why subscribers cancel their Booksy subscription. The specific decision this research should inform has not yet been defined — this should be confirmed before the guide is finalised.

Participant profile (suggested): Users who cancelled their subscription in the last 90 days. Target 6–8 interviews for pattern saturation. Consider adding 3–4 active long-term subscribers for contrast if resources allow.

Interview guide (starter draft — review before use):

Warm-up:
- "How do you usually consume books — audiobooks, reading, something else?"
- "What made you try an audiobook subscription in the first place?"

Context-setting:
- "Walk me through what happened when you first started using Booksy. What were you hoping it would be like?"
- "How did you choose what to listen to in the first few weeks?"

Core questions:
- "Was there a moment when you started feeling like the subscription wasn't worth it? Tell me about that."
- "What would have made you more likely to stay?"
- "What do you use now instead of Booksy when you want to listen to something?"

Wrap-up:
- "What would make an audiobook app worth subscribing to long-term for you?"

Note: This guide is a starting point. Before running interviews, confirm:
- The specific decision these interviews will inform
- Whether active subscribers should be included for contrast
- Note-taking and synthesis protocol

Number of interviews: Recommend 6–8 cancelled subscribers as a minimum. Add 3–4 active subscribers if possible — the contrast often produces the most useful findings. Finalise count once research goal is confirmed.

Missing before finalising: Specific decision this research must inform; whether to include active subscribers; note-taking assignment; synthesis timeline and format.
