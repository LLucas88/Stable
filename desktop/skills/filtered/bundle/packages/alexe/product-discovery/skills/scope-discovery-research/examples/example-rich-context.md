## Input

Product: Swapsy — a B2C peer-to-peer resale marketplace for secondhand clothing and accessories, operating via a mobile app.

Context: The team has identified that sellers who list 5+ items in their first month retain at high rates, but only 12% of new sellers reach that threshold. Most list 1–2 items and never return. The team needs to decide between two possible investment directions: (A) simplify the listing flow to reduce friction, or (B) introduce a seller onboarding programme that incentivises the first 5 listings. Research must produce a clear recommendation. Timeline: 4 weeks. Resources: 1 product researcher, 1 part-time designer for concept testing, access to seller base for recruiting.

## Expected output

Research goal: Understand why new sellers stop listing after their first 1–2 items, and determine whether the barrier is listing friction (too much effort per item) or motivational (unclear value of listing more), to decide between simplifying the listing flow or building a seller onboarding programme.

Key research questions (prioritised):
1. What motivates a new seller to list their first item — and does that motivation carry over to a second and third listing?
2. What does the listing process feel like from the seller's perspective — where do they hesitate, abandon, or feel uncertain?
3. What happens after a first listing: does the seller check for interest, wait passively, or forget about it?
4. What would make a seller list 5 items instead of 1 — incentive, easier flow, social proof, or something else?
5. What are sellers doing with the items they don't list on Swapsy — keeping them, donating, using a different platform?

Known vs. unknown:
- Known: 12% of new sellers reach 5+ listings in the first month; sellers who reach this threshold retain well; listing flow has 6 steps including photo upload, description, pricing, and category selection
- Unknown: Whether the drop-off is caused by listing effort, unclear pricing, lack of early buyer interest, or motivational factors unrelated to the product; whether the 5-listing threshold is a cause of retention or a proxy for seller motivation

Methods per question:
- Q1–Q3: Semi-structured interviews (30–40 min) with new sellers — best method for understanding motivation, emotional context, and decision points
- Q4: Concept test within interviews — show two concepts (simplified flow vs. onboarding programme) and observe reaction; not to pick a winner but to understand what resonates and why
- Q5: Closing question within interviews — indirect inquiry to surface competing channels and disposal alternatives

Participant criteria:
- New Swapsy sellers who registered in the last 90 days
- Group A (4–5 interviews): Listed 1–2 items and did not list again within 30 days
- Group B (3–4 interviews): Listed 5+ items in their first month (retained sellers for contrast)
- All participants: completed at least one listing with photos
- Recruiting via email to recent seller cohort; offer a small credit incentive for inactive sellers

Timeline:
- Week 1: Recruit participants; prepare interview guide and concept materials
- Week 2: Conduct 8–9 interviews
- Week 3: Synthesise findings using affinity mapping; identify key themes
- Week 4: Produce findings document and decision recommendation; present to product and design team

Output format: Written research summary with top insights, a clear recommendation on which investment direction the evidence supports, and specific design implications for the chosen direction.

Risk and contingency plan: If inactive sellers are difficult to recruit via email, supplement with in-app survey targeting sellers with exactly 1 listing and no activity in the last 14 days. If fewer than 4 inactive sellers participate, treat findings as directional only and flag the limitation.
