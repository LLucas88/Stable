## Input
I need a launch checklist for a new feature.

## Expected output
Provisional checklist template:

What is known:
- A feature is being launched

What must be confirmed before a useful checklist can be built:
- What feature or product is being launched
- Which teams are involved
- What the launch scope is (beta, soft launch, general availability)
- What the launch timeline is

Suggested next inputs:
- feature or product name and brief description
- list of teams involved in the launch
- planned launch date or timeline
- launch scope (who is the target audience at launch)

Confidence level:
Provisional
