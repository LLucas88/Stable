## Input
Design packaging tiers for my product.

## Expected output
Packaging tier framework (provisional):

What is known:
- A product needs packaging tiers

What must be confirmed before a tier structure can be designed:
- What the product is and what capabilities it has to tier
- Who the target customer segments are (at minimum 2-3 distinct profiles)
- What the business model is (freemium, self-serve SaaS, enterprise, etc.)
- What the value metric is — what customers get more of as they pay more

Suggested next inputs:
- product description and list of features or capabilities
- description of 2-3 customer segments with different needs or budgets
- business model and current pricing (if any exists)
- reason for adding or redesigning tiers (acquisition, conversion, revenue)

Confidence level:
Provisional
