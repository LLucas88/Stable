## Input
We have an EdTech platform for learning Spanish. We want to create messaging for a new live tutoring feature. Users are adults who want to practice speaking. We think our main advantage is the price — cheaper than competitors.

## Expected output
Positioning statement (provisional):
For adult learners who want to practice speaking Spanish, our live tutoring feature offers affordable access to real tutors — at a price point below the leading alternatives.

Value proposition (provisional):
Practice speaking Spanish with a real tutor, at a fraction of the cost of traditional online tutoring.

Key messages:
1. Real practice, real people
   The only way to actually improve speaking is to speak. Live tutors give you the conversation practice apps can't.

2. Accessible price point
   Specific pricing not provided — if the advantage is cost, the messaging needs the actual number to be credible.

3. On your schedule
   Assumed flexibility — needs confirmation whether this is a real differentiator.

Tagline option:
"Speak Spanish. With a real person."

Messaging hierarchy:
Headline: Practice speaking Spanish with a real tutor.
Subheadline: Live sessions, flexible schedule, prices that don't require a language school budget.
Body: [Needs proof points — specific pricing, tutor vetting, session format]

What to test with users:
- Whether "cheaper than competitors" is the primary reason adult learners choose this vs. app-only practice
- Whether users care more about price or tutor quality
- What "adult learner" means in practice — professional context, travel, heritage speaker?

Missing information:
- Actual price point and competitor comparison
- Tutor selection and quality signals
- Session format (scheduled vs. on-demand, session length)
- Whether users have been researched or this is internal assumption

Confidence level:
Provisional
