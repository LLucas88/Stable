## Input
Product: a receipt scanning and expense categorization feature in our freelance finance app.
Target user: freelancers and solo consultants who invoice clients and need to track deductible expenses.

The core problem: they collect receipts across email, photo, and paper. At tax time, they spend hours (sometimes a full day) sorting and categorizing everything. Many miss deductions because they can't find receipts.

What the feature does: take a photo of any receipt, AI extracts vendor, amount, date, and category. Receipts sync to a folder organized by month and category. Export to CSV or directly to their accountant.

Why it's better than alternatives:
- Generic expense apps (Expensify, Ramp) are built for teams with approval workflows — overkill for solo users
- Google Photos or folders: manual, no extraction, no categorization
- Doing it in a spreadsheet: manual, error-prone

User quote from research: "The day before my accountant meeting I want to die a little."

## Expected output
Value proposition statement:
[App name] helps freelancers eliminate tax-season receipt chaos by automatically extracting and categorizing expenses from photos — unlike team expense tools, it's built for one person with no setup overhead.

Target user:
Freelancers and solo consultants who manage their own expenses and file business taxes independently.

Core job or problem addressed:
Organizing deductible expenses across the year without losing receipts or spending a day sorting them before tax filing.

Outcome delivered:
Receipt captured and categorized in under 10 seconds. Tax-ready export when needed — no spreadsheet required.

Differentiation from alternatives:
Team expense tools have approval workflows, multi-user setups, and company card integrations that are irrelevant for solo users. Folders and spreadsheets require manual work with no extraction. This feature is designed specifically for one-person use: snap, done, export.

Specificity test:
This statement would not fit a team expense tool, a generic accounting platform, or a receipt storage app — it is specific to the solo freelancer context and the tax-prep job.

Alternative formulations:
1. "Snap your receipt. We'll handle the rest. Tax-ready exports whenever you need them."
2. "The expense tracker that doesn't assume you have an accountant on staff."
3. "One photo. Categorized. Ready for your accountant."

Language to avoid:
- "Expense management" (sounds corporate, not how freelancers talk)
- "Streamline your workflow" (generic)
- "Powerful AI" (says nothing about the outcome)
