## Input
We have a productivity app and we're trying to figure out pricing. It's a subscription model. We think we should add a free tier but we're not sure. We have some paid users now but the conversion from free trial isn't great.

## Expected output
Value metric:
Not confirmed. For a productivity app, likely candidates are: active users, number of projects/workspaces, storage, or feature access. The right value metric depends on what users get more value from as usage grows.

Pricing model options (provisional):

Option 1: Freemium
- Free tier with limited features or capacity
- One or more paid tiers
- Could address the low trial conversion if the free tier is well-scoped
- Risk: if free is too generous, paid conversion stays low

Option 2: Extended trial
- Longer free trial (30 or 60 days) instead of a permanent free tier
- Simpler to implement; doesn't create a permanent free user base
- Risk: still loses users who want ongoing free access

Option 3: Lower price point on entry plan
- If price sensitivity is the issue, reducing the entry price may convert more trials than adding a free tier
- Needs validation before assuming a free tier is the right answer

Recommended direction:
Cannot recommend a specific structure without knowing: what "not great" conversion means in numbers, why users are not converting (price? feature gaps? habit not formed?), and what the current pricing and trial terms are.

Validation steps:
- Interview 5-10 recent trial non-converters: price, missing features, or never formed the habit?
- Check trial usage data: do non-converters actually use the product during trial?
- Define the value metric before designing tiers

Risks and open questions:
- Free tier can hurt revenue if conversion triggers aren't designed carefully
- "Productivity app" covers a wide range — specifics matter for tier design

Confidence level:
Provisional
