# Example: Poor Context

## Input

"How do I know if my product has product-market fit?"

## Output

### Status
No product context provided. Cannot perform a fit assessment without knowing the product, segment, and available signals.

### What is needed to run this skill
1. What is the product and what does it do?
2. Who is the target user or customer?
3. What signals do you have? (even rough: "users keep coming back", "high churn", "NPS score")

### Framework: What signals to look for
While waiting for context, here is the minimum set of signals needed for an honest PMF assessment:

| Signal type | What to look for |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Retention | Are users coming back after 30, 60, 90 days? |
| Qualitative resonance | Do users say they'd be very disappointed to lose the product? |
| Organic growth | Are users referring others without being pushed? |
| Engagement depth | Are users using core features repeatedly, not just once? |
| Expansion | Are existing customers expanding usage or spend over time? |

### What happens next
Share any of the above signals and the product/segment context, and this skill will produce a structured fit assessment.
