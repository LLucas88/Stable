# Example: Rich Context

## Input

**Product areas:**
1. Core pipeline management (mature, high retention)
2. AI-assisted deal scoring (in development, early traction)
3. CRM integrations marketplace (low engagement, high maintenance cost)
4. Mobile app (requested by enterprise customers, not yet built)

**Business goals (this year):**
- Grow ARR by 40%
- Improve net revenue retention to 120%
- Land 3 enterprise logos

**Constraints:** 3 product teams, engineering capacity is fixed for 6 months

## Output

### Portfolio map

| Area | Strategic class | Rationale |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Core pipeline management | Core — defend and extend | Foundation of retention; must stay excellent |
| AI-assisted deal scoring | Growth — invest | Early traction, differentiator, supports ARR and NRR goals |
| Mobile app | Growth — accelerate | Direct ask from enterprise segment; blocks 3 logo goal |
| CRM integrations marketplace | Explore — rationalize | High maintenance, low engagement; ROI unclear |

### Prioritization rationale
Enterprise logo goal and NRR target make mobile app and AI scoring the top investments.
Core pipeline must be maintained but not require major new investment.
CRM marketplace should be rationalized — reduce maintenance burden before deciding to build or kill.

### High-level roadmap structure

**Now (0–3 months)**
- Stabilize and maintain core pipeline
- Accelerate mobile app MVP for enterprise pilots
- Continue AI scoring development

**Next (3–6 months)**
- Ship mobile app to enterprise pilots
- Expand AI scoring to all paid accounts
- Audit and reduce CRM marketplace scope

**Later (6–12 months)**
- Evaluate CRM marketplace: expand, partner, or sunset
- AI scoring v2 based on early data

### Trade-offs
- CRM marketplace is being deprioritized — existing users must be informed
- Mobile app is accelerated at the cost of other enhancements to core pipeline

### Key alignment decisions needed
- Does leadership accept CRM marketplace deprioritization?
- Is the mobile app timeline (3 months to MVP) realistic with current engineering capacity?

### Confidence level
Context-informed — strong input, but capacity estimates need engineering validation.
