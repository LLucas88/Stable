# Example: Poor Context

## Input

"Help me build a roadmap."

## Output

### Status
Cannot build a roadmap without context. A roadmap is a strategic communication tool — it only makes sense relative to goals, constraints, and product areas.

### What is needed
1. What are you building? (product type, stage, user base)
2. What are your top 1–3 business goals this period?
3. What product areas or initiatives are in scope?
4. What are your constraints? (team size, time horizon)

### What happens next
Once goals, areas, and constraints are known, this skill will produce a portfolio map and a now/next/later roadmap structure with explicit trade-off rationale.
