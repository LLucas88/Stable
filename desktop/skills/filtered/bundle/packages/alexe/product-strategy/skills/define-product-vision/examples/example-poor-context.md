# Example: Poor Context

## Input

"We need a product vision for our new platform."

## Output

### Status
Insufficient context to produce a meaningful vision.

### Discovery questions

Before producing a vision, the following must be established:

1. What product or platform is this? What does it do?
2. Who are the primary users or customers?
3. What problem does the product solve for them?
4. What is the business goal — growth, retention, new market, cost reduction?
5. What is the rough time horizon — 1 year, 3 years, 5+ years?
6. Is there an existing vision that needs to be updated, or is this net new?

### What happens next
Once at least the product area, target user, and business goal are known, this skill can produce a provisional vision.
A full vision requires all five inputs above.
