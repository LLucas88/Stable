# Example: Light Context

## Input

**Product:** HR software for small businesses
**Target segment:** HR managers at companies with 20–100 employees
**Competitors:** "There's BambooHR, and some other tools I've heard of."

## Output

### Status
Partial context available. Provisional differentiation framework provided; specifics require more input.

### Known competitive landscape (partial)
- BambooHR: well-known SMB HR platform, strong brand, feature-rich, higher price point for small teams
- "Other tools" — needs identification. Common alternatives: Gusto, Rippling, Personio, Lattice

### What's missing to produce a real differentiation statement
1. What are your key capabilities or unique features?
2. Why have past customers chosen you over BambooHR or others?
3. What do customers say you do better or worse?
4. What is your pricing position relative to competitors?

### Provisional differentiation areas to explore
Based on common patterns for this segment:
- Price: are you cheaper for 20–100 person companies?
- Simplicity: are you easier to set up and use?
- Specific capability: do you do payroll, compliance, onboarding, or performance better?
- Customer support: do you offer better support for non-expert HR managers?

### Confidence level
Provisional — need competitive landscape and product capability data to proceed.
