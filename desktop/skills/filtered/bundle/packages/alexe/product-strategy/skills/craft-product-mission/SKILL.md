# Craft Product Mission

## Purpose
Help teams articulate a mission statement that anchors day-to-day decisions: what the product does, for whom, and what value it delivers right now.

## Skill type
Conceptual skill

## Use this skill when
- The team confuses vision and mission or uses them interchangeably
- A mission statement is too vague to guide product trade-offs
- A new product area needs a mission before planning begins
- Team alignment breaks down because there is no shared "what we do and for whom"

## Do not use this skill when
- The goal is long-term aspirational direction (use define-product-vision)
- The goal is competitive positioning (use define-strategic-positioning)

## Required inputs
- Target user or customer segment
- Core value the product delivers
- Product area or type

## Optional inputs
- Existing mission or vision drafts
- Business model context
- OKRs or strategic goals

## Upstream context
Works best when:
- Vision is defined or being developed in parallel
- Target segment is identified

## If upstream context is missing
Draft a provisional mission and list what must be validated before it can be used to make decisions.

## Downstream handoff
Output can feed:
- build-portfolio-roadmap-strategy (mission constrains what goes on the roadmap)
- set-goals-okrs-kpis (OKRs should map to mission)
- develop-positioning-messaging (mission is the backbone of external messaging)

## Instructions
1. Identify who the product serves (specific segment, not everyone).
2. Identify the core job or problem the product addresses.
3. Identify the value delivered (outcome, not feature).
4. Draft a mission statement: "[Product] helps [who] [do what] so they can [outcome]."
5. Test the draft: is it specific enough to say no to things? Would it exclude irrelevant features?
6. Flag what is still assumed vs. validated.

## Output
Provide:
- Mission statement
- Who it serves
- Core job or problem addressed
- Value delivered
- Specificity test: what would this mission say no to?
- Confidence level: provisional / context-informed / validated
- Open questions

## Risks / caveats
- "Helps everyone do everything better" is not a mission
- Mission must be present-tense and achievable — vision is aspirational, mission is operational
- If the mission can't be used to reject bad ideas, it's too vague
