# Example: Rich Context

## Input

**Business goals:** Reach $50M ARR in 3 years; expand from SMB to mid-market; maintain 85%+ gross retention.
**Strategic bets under consideration:**
1. AI-powered forecasting module (new capability for mid-market)
2. Native mobile app (current product is web-only)
3. Two-way CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
4. White-label offering for channel partners
5. Self-serve onboarding redesign (reduce time-to-value for SMB)

**Resource constraints:** 3 product teams, 18-month runway before next funding round.
**Risk tolerance:** Leadership wants 1–2 bold bets and prefers reversible investments where possible.

## Output

### Bet inventory

| Bet | Goal | Hypothesis | Investment (est.) | Payoff potential |
|-----|------|------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| AI forecasting | Win mid-market | Mid-market buyers won't convert without planning/forecasting features | High (new ML infra) | High — unlocks $15K+ ACV accounts |
| Mobile app | Reduce churn | Field users churn because product is desktop-only | Medium | Medium — retention, not expansion |
| CRM integrations | Accelerate mid-market sales | Deals stall because we can't sync with Salesforce | Medium | High — removes a blocker in enterprise sales |
| White-label | New revenue channel | Partners will resell if margins are attractive | High (GTM + eng) | Medium — uncertain partner demand |
| Self-serve onboarding | SMB activation | Improving onboarding will increase 30-day activation from 40% to 65% | Low | Medium — reduces churn at SMB tier |

### Assessment matrix

| Bet | Strategic alignment | Potential value | Confidence | Reversibility |
|-----|--------------------|-----------------|-----------:|--------------|
| AI forecasting | High (mid-market) | High | Low | Low |
| Mobile app | Medium (retention) | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| CRM integrations | High (mid-market) | High | High | High |
| White-label | Low (new motion) | Medium | Low | Low |
| Self-serve onboarding | Medium (SMB health) | Medium | High | High |

### Portfolio classification

| Bet | Classification |
|-----|---------------|
| CRM integrations | **Core** — high confidence, removes known blocker, reversible |
| Self-serve onboarding | **Core** — high confidence, measurable, low cost |
| AI forecasting | **Growth** — high potential, lower confidence, build toward H2 |
| Mobile app | **Growth** — retention lever; validate with churn data before committing |
| White-label | **Explore** — interesting but no demand signal; requires kill criteria |

### Prioritization recommendation

**Fund now:**
1. CRM integrations — highest confidence, directly unblocks mid-market pipeline. Assign 1 team.
2. Self-serve onboarding — fast, measurable, addresses SMB churn. Assign shared capacity.

**Build toward (H2 start):**
3. AI forecasting — required for mid-market differentiation; begin technical scoping now, build in H2.

**Validate before committing:**
4. Mobile app — run a 6-week churn analysis to confirm field users are churning for this reason before resourcing.

**Deprioritize:**
5. White-label — no validated partner demand; revisit if inbound requests exceed 10 qualified inquiries in 6 months.

### What is being deprioritized and why
White-label is deprioritized because it requires building a new GTM motion (partner sales) with no validated demand, while consuming significant engineering and business development resources. This is a distraction from the core mid-market move.

### Review triggers and milestones
- CRM integration: measure deal velocity change after 90 days
- AI forecasting: reassess scope after technical spike (6 weeks)
- Mobile app: churn analysis results in 6 weeks trigger go/no-go
- White-label: revisit at Q3 planning if partner interest materializes
