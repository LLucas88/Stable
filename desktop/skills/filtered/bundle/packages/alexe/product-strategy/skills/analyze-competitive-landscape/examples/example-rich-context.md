# Example: Rich Context

## Input

**Product:** AI-powered legal contract review tool for in-house legal teams
**Target segment:** In-house legal teams at companies with 500–5,000 employees
**Known competitors:** Ironclad, Kira, Luminance, LexCheck, manual review by outside counsel
**Recent developments:** Several players raised significant Series B/C in 2024; Microsoft Copilot added basic contract summarization; law firms are building their own AI tools
**Win/loss data:** Win vs. Kira on speed and UX; lose to Ironclad when procurement controls the purchase; lose to "we'll build it ourselves" at large tech companies
**Customer feedback:** Top alternative mentioned by churned customers is "reverted to outside counsel for high-stakes contracts"

## Output

### Competitor inventory

**Direct competitors** (same category, same job):
- Ironclad — contract lifecycle management (CLM) platform; broader than just review
- Kira Systems — AI contract analysis; enterprise-focused, attorney-heavy buyer
- Luminance — AI for legal; European-origin, strong in M&A due diligence
- LexCheck — contract redlining and negotiation AI; narrow focus

**Indirect competitors** (different category, same job):
- Microsoft Copilot / Legal add-ons — generic AI applied to contracts; shallow but ubiquitous
- Outside counsel (law firms) — traditional alternative for high-stakes review
- Internal legal ops teams doing manual review (status quo)

**Status quo alternatives:**
- Manual review with tracked-changes in Word
- Templated playbooks reviewed by junior associates

### Competitor profiles

| Competitor | Target segment | Value proposition | Key capabilities | Apparent strategy |
|------------|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Ironclad | Mid-market to enterprise, procurement-led | End-to-end CLM, not just review | Workflow automation, repository, e-sign | Platform land-and-expand via procurement |
| Kira | Enterprise, GC/partner-led | Deep AI accuracy for complex contracts | Clause extraction, due diligence | Accuracy-first, premium enterprise |
| Luminance | Enterprise, M&A and legal ops | AI trained on legal language globally | Due diligence, regulatory compliance | Geographic expansion, large deal focus |
| LexCheck | Mid-market, in-house legal | Fast redlining and negotiation guidance | Playbook-based redlining | Narrow, fast, workflow-integrated |

### Competitive dimension map

| Dimension | Our product | Ironclad | Kira | Luminance | LexCheck |
|-----------|-------------|----------|------|-----------|----------|
| Review speed | High | Medium | Medium | Medium | High |
| AI accuracy (complex contracts) | Medium | Low | High | High | Medium |
| UX / ease of use | High | Medium | Low | Low | High |
| CLM / workflow breadth | Low | High | Medium | Medium | Low |
| Price | Medium | High | High | High | Medium |
| Buyer (legal vs. procurement) | Legal | Procurement | Legal | Legal | Legal |

### Areas of commoditization vs. differentiation opportunity

**Commoditizing:**
- Basic contract summarization (Copilot, GPT wrappers are making this a commodity)
- Standard clause extraction for common contract types (NDAs, MSAs)

**Differentiation opportunity:**
- Deep accuracy on high-stakes, non-standard contracts (M&A, IP, regulatory)
- Workflow integration with in-house legal team tools (not just the contract itself)
- Trust and auditability: showing why the AI flagged something, not just what it flagged
- Serving the in-house legal buyer directly (vs. procurement-controlled CLM)

### Competitive threats

**Short-term:**
- Microsoft Copilot expanding legal templates — erodes the low-complexity review use case
- Ironclad moving down-market with lighter SKUs could compete in our segment

**Long-term:**
- Law firms building proprietary AI and offering it as part of outside counsel services
- Large tech companies building internal tools and churning out of all third-party options

### Strategic opportunities
1. Own the in-house legal buyer — most CLM platforms are won/lost in procurement; we can build direct legal team loyalty
2. Accuracy + explainability for high-stakes contracts — the one area where AI trust remains low and the value of getting it right is highest
3. Integrations with legal workflow tools (e-billing, matter management) rather than trying to be a CLM

### Confidence level and data currency
High confidence on direct competitor profiles (based on win/loss data, customer interviews). Medium confidence on Microsoft Copilot threat trajectory. Recommend refreshing competitive data every 6 months given pace of AI development.
