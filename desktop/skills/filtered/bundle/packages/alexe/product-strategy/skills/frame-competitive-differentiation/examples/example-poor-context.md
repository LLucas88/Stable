# Example: Poor Context

## Input

"How is my product different from competitors?"

## Output

### Status
Cannot assess differentiation without knowing the product, segment, or competitors.

### What is needed
1. What does your product do?
2. Who is it for?
3. Who do you see as your main competitors?
4. What do customers say when they choose you — or choose not to?

### What happens next
Once the product, segment, and at least 2 competitors are known, this skill will produce a differentiation map and a differentiation statement.
