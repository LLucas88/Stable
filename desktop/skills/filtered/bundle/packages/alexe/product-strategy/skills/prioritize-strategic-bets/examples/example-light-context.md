# Example: Light Context

## Input

**Business goal:** Grow revenue and improve retention.
**Bets under consideration:**
1. Build a mobile app
2. Add AI features
3. Expand to international markets
4. Improve onboarding

**Resource constraints:** Small team, limited budget.

## Output

### Status
Insufficient detail to produce a reliable prioritization. The bets are named but not defined, and the strategy context is too thin to evaluate alignment. The output below is a structured starting point, not a recommendation.

### What is missing

To prioritize these bets, the following is needed per bet:
- What specific problem does this bet solve?
- Who benefits, and how does it connect to revenue or retention?
- What is the rough effort (team-months, cost)?
- What is the confidence that this bet will work?

### Provisional framing (directional only)

| Bet | Why it might matter | Why it might not |
|-----|--------------------|--------------------|
| Mobile app | Users may be churning due to no mobile access | May be building infra for a non-existent need |
| AI features | High market interest; potential differentiator | "AI features" is not a strategy — needs to be specific |
| International | Revenue diversification | Requires localization, support, legal — significant overhead |
| Improve onboarding | High-confidence retention lever if onboarding is a known gap | If onboarding is fine, no impact |

### Discovery questions before prioritization

1. What do churn interviews say? What is the stated reason for leaving?
2. What does usage data show — where do users drop off?
3. Are there deal blockers in the sales process that map to any of these bets?
4. What does "AI features" mean specifically — what job would AI do for users?
5. What markets are being considered for international expansion, and is there existing inbound demand?

### What happens next
Once each bet has a clear problem statement, hypothesis, and effort estimate, a full prioritization can be run. Use the assess-product-market-fit and formulate-experiment-hypothesis skills to sharpen individual bets before scoring them.
