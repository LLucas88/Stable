# Example: Poor Context

## Input

"We have a few big ideas. Help us figure out which ones to pursue."

## Output

### Status
Insufficient context to prioritize strategic bets.

### What is missing

Prioritizing bets requires:
1. **The bets themselves** — each bet needs a name, a goal, and a rough description of what it is
2. **Business goals or strategic objectives** — bets are evaluated against what the organization is trying to achieve
3. **Resource constraints** — teams, budget, and time horizon determine what's even possible to pursue

Without these, any prioritization would be arbitrary.

### Discovery questions

Before prioritizing, establish:

1. What are the specific bets or initiatives being considered? (Name and one-sentence description each)
2. What is the business trying to achieve in the next 12–24 months? (Revenue, growth, retention, market expansion?)
3. How many teams or how much budget is available to invest in new bets?
4. What is the risk tolerance — does leadership want high-confidence bets, bold bets, or a mix?
5. Are there bets already in flight that need to be accounted for?

### What happens next

Once the bets are named and the strategy context is clear, this skill can:
- Build a bet inventory with goals, hypotheses, and payoff potential
- Score each bet on alignment, value, confidence, and reversibility
- Classify bets as core / growth / explore
- Produce a prioritization recommendation with rationale
