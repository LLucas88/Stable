## Input
Company strategy this year: double down on SMB segment, reduce enterprise dependency, hit $5M ARR. The three company OKRs are: (1) grow SMB MRR to $3.5M by Dec, (2) reduce SMB churn below 3% monthly, (3) launch 2 SMB-specific features.

My team's OKRs:
- O1: Improve product onboarding — KR: 60% of new users complete onboarding in the first session
- O2: Build enterprise reporting dashboard — KR: ship reporting v1 to top 10 enterprise accounts by Q3
- O3: Reduce support ticket volume — KR: cut support tickets per user per month by 20%

We're a product team of 4 (2 engineers, 1 designer, me as PM). We also have a sales team focused on SMB and an enterprise CS team.

## Expected output
Goal-to-strategy mapping:

| Team Goal | Company Priority | Alignment |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Improve onboarding (60% completion) | SMB growth, SMB churn reduction | Strong — SMB onboarding friction directly affects activation and churn |
| Build enterprise reporting dashboard | None | Weak — company strategy explicitly de-emphasizes enterprise |
| Reduce support ticket volume | Indirect SMB churn benefit | Partial — relevant if support issues drive SMB churn, but not confirmed |

Goals with strong alignment:
- O1 (onboarding) maps well to both SMB MRR growth and churn reduction. New SMB users who don't complete onboarding churn faster.

Goals with weak or no alignment:
- O2 (enterprise reporting) is directly misaligned with company strategy. The company is reducing enterprise dependency, but this goal invests capacity in the top 10 enterprise accounts. This is either legacy commitment that needs to be surfaced, or a goal that should be cut.

Strategy areas not covered:
- The company wants 2 SMB-specific features. Neither O1 nor O3 clearly corresponds to a differentiated SMB feature — O1 is a general onboarding improvement. If the SMB feature requirement is real, this team may need an explicit goal for it.

Cross-team conflicts:
- Enterprise CS team likely expects the reporting dashboard. If O2 is deprioritized, this needs explicit communication — not just a silent roadmap change.

Recommended adjustments:
1. Keep O1 as primary — strengthen it by making the SMB segment explicit ("60% of SMB new users" not just "new users")
2. Sunset or transfer O2 — if enterprise commitments are already made, escalate to leadership to decide whether to honor them or re-negotiate; don't quietly carry it
3. Reframe O3 to connect support tickets to SMB churn specifically — if support issues are causing SMB churn, this is high-value; if it's mostly enterprise support volume, it's misaligned
4. Add an explicit OKR for an SMB-differentiated capability if that company OKR is real and not owned elsewhere

Alignment confidence level:
O1: strong. O2: misaligned — needs decision. O3: partial — needs data to confirm connection to SMB churn.
