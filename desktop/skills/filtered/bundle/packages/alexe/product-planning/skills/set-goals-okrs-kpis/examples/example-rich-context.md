## Input
We're a B2C fitness app, subscription-based. 100k MAU, ~12% monthly churn. Revenue is flat despite user growth because activation is weak — only 31% of new signups complete the first workout within 7 days, and those who don't are churning at 2x the rate of activated users.

The company's strategic priority this year is improving retention and reducing CAC payback period. Our team owns the new user experience from signup to first value.

Time horizon: Q2 (13 weeks). We have 2 engineers, 1 designer. No formal OKRs yet at the team level.

## Expected output
Objectives (2):

**Objective 1:** Get new users to their first real fitness win before they give up

Key Results:
- KR1: Increase 7-day first-workout completion rate from 31% to 48% by end of Q2
- KR2: Reduce D7 churn for new users from current baseline to within 10 percentage points of activated-user churn rate
- KR3: Increase % of users who set a fitness goal during onboarding from unknown baseline (instrument first, target by week 4)

**Objective 2:** Make activation work for the acquisition mix we actually have, not just our best-case users

Key Results:
- KR1: Achieve activation rates above 40% across all top 3 acquisition sources (paid social, organic search, referral)
- KR2: Reduce time-to-first-workout from median unknown to under 10 minutes of in-app time

---

KPIs for ongoing health monitoring:
- 7-day activation rate (first workout completed) — weekly
- D7 and D30 churn by activation status — weekly
- Median time-to-first-workout (in-app minutes) — weekly
- Onboarding completion rate by step — weekly

Baseline values:
- 7-day activation: 31%
- D7 churn (unactivated): approximately 2x D7 churn (activated) — exact baseline TBD

Assumptions behind KRs:
- "First workout" = any workout session of 10+ minutes completed within 7 days of signup
- Activation-to-retention correlation is causal, not just correlated — this needs validation
- 13 weeks is enough to run at least one meaningful A/B test and ship a follow-up iteration

Risks to goal achievement:
- Engineering capacity: with 2 engineers, can likely ship 1-2 meaningful onboarding changes, not a full redesign
- Baseline data gaps: some metrics require instrumentation before meaningful targets can be set
- Acquisition mix shifts: if paid campaigns change traffic composition mid-quarter, segment-level KRs may be distorted

Confidence level:
Context-informed
