## Input
Help me run a retro.

## Expected output
A retrospective needs a scope and a team before it can be structured.

What is needed:
- What event or period is being reviewed? (Sprint, quarter, launch, experiment, incident)
- Who is the team? (How many people, what roles)

Optional but makes the retro more useful:
- What data or outcomes are available? (Did you hit goals? Any incidents?)
- Were there prior retro action items? If so, what were they and were they completed?
- Is there a specific problem the team wants to address in this retro?

Without a scope, the retro has no anchor. Without any outcome data, observations will be impressionistic and hard to act on.

Suggested next inputs:
- "We're doing a retro on [sprint / Q1 / the launch of X]"
- "The team is [size and roles]"
- "The main thing that went wrong was [observation]" — optional but helpful
