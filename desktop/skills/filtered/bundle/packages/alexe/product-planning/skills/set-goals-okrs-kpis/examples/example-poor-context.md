## Input
Help me write OKRs.

## Expected output
Provisional hypothesis template:
Objective: [qualitative, directional goal — what your team is trying to achieve]
Key Result 1: [specific, measurable outcome — not a task or output]
Key Result 2: [specific, measurable outcome]
Key Result 3: [specific, measurable outcome — optional]

What is known:
- You want to write OKRs

What is assumed:
- Nothing reliable yet

What must be confirmed before useful OKRs can be written:
- What product area or team is this for?
- What is the strategic priority or business problem being addressed?
- What time horizon — quarter, half-year, year?
- What are current metric baselines? (Can't write measurable KRs without them)
- Are there company-level OKRs these team OKRs should connect to?

Suggested next inputs:
- Product area or team scope
- The one business or user problem you most need to solve next quarter
- Any metric you already track (with its current value)
- The company's stated priorities

Confidence level:
Provisional
