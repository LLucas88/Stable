## Input
Prioritize my backlog.

## Expected output
Prioritization requires at least two inputs: the list of items to prioritize and the goal that priority should serve.

What is needed:
- The list of features, initiatives, or requests you want to prioritize (even rough descriptions)
- The goal these items are competing for — what outcome should the highest-priority items move?

Without a goal, there is no prioritization — there is only sorting by preference.

Optional but useful:
- Effort estimates or relative complexity
- Any data on user demand, frequency of requests, or impact signals
- Team capacity (how many engineers, what time horizon)

Suggested next inputs:
- "Here are the items: [list]"
- "The goal this quarter is [metric or outcome]"
