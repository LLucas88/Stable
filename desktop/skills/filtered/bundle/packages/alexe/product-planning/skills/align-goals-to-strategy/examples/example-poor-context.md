## Input
Are my goals aligned to strategy?

## Expected output
Alignment cannot be assessed without both sides of the comparison.

What is known:
- You want to check goal alignment

What is missing:
- What are your team's current goals or OKRs?
- What is the company strategy or company-level priorities?

Without both inputs, there is nothing to compare.

Suggested next inputs:
- Your team's OKRs or KPIs (even rough drafts)
- Company strategy: top priorities, strategic bets, or company-level OKRs
- If company strategy isn't formally documented, describe what leadership says the company is focused on this year

Once both are provided, the alignment check can identify:
- Which goals map to company priorities
- Which goals have no strategic connection
- Which company priorities have no team coverage
- Cross-team conflicts if any are visible
