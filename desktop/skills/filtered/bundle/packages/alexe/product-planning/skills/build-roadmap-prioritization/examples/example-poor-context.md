## Input
Help me build a product roadmap.

## Expected output
A roadmap requires two things before it can be structured: a list of initiatives to prioritize, and goals that those initiatives should serve.

Without these inputs, any roadmap produced would be a template, not a plan.

What is needed:
- List of features, initiatives, or requests being considered (even rough)
- The goals or OKRs this roadmap should move forward
- Time horizon (quarter, half-year, annual?)
- Team capacity (rough: how many engineers, designers?)

Without goals, there's no way to sequence — prioritization becomes opinion, not strategy.
Without a list of initiatives, there's nothing to structure.

Suggested next inputs:
- What problem is the team trying to solve in the next planning cycle?
- What is the one metric that would move if the roadmap succeeded?
- What initiatives or ideas are already on the table?
