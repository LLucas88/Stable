## Input
What are the risks of our initiative?

## Expected output
Risk mitigation planning requires knowing what the initiative is.

What is known:
- An initiative exists with risks to plan for

What must be confirmed:
- What is the initiative? (scope, timeline, team)
- What is the goal of the initiative?
- What are the key dependencies?
- Are there known concerns or constraints?

Without this, a risk inventory produces generic categories (technical, market, execution, legal, organizational) applied to an unknown context — not the specific risks that matter for this initiative.

Suggested next inputs:
- Initiative description and scope
- Timeline and key dependencies
- Known concerns or risks already identified

Confidence level:
Provisional
