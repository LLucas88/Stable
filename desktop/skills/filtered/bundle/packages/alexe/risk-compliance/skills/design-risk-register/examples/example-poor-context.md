## Input
We need a risk register.

## Expected output
A risk register requires knowing what initiative it covers and what risks have been identified.

What is known:
- A risk register is needed

What must be confirmed:
- What initiative or program does the register cover?
- What risks have already been identified? (pre-mortem, risk assessment, team input)
- What is the scope and timeline of the initiative?
- Who is the intended owner of the register?

A risk register without known risks is a blank template. A risk register without named owners for each risk is a document that won't drive action.

If no risks have been identified yet, run a pre-mortem first — it will surface the realistic failure modes that the register should track.

Suggested next inputs:
- Initiative or program description
- Known risks (even a rough list)
- Owners available (who can be responsible for individual risks)

Confidence level:
Provisional
