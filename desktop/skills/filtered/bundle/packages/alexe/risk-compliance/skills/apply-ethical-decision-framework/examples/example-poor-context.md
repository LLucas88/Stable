## Input
Is our product ethical?

## Expected output
Ethical evaluation requires a specific decision, feature, or product element to assess.

What is known:
- There is a question about ethics

What must be confirmed before assessment:
- What specific product decision, feature, or business practice is being evaluated?
- Who are the affected user groups?
- What is the business goal the decision serves?
- Are there known concerns or objections that prompted this question?

"Is our product ethical" is too broad to answer usefully. Almost every product has some ethical tensions. Identifying them requires examining specific decisions and their effects on specific user groups.

Suggested approach if you're starting broadly:
Identify the highest-risk decisions in the product — typically: decisions that directly affect user wellbeing, decisions that collect sensitive data, decisions where business incentives and user interests might diverge. Start with one of those and run the evaluation from there.

- User groups affected
- Known concerns or questions
