## Input
Can you do a pre-mortem for our project?

## Expected output
A pre-mortem requires knowing what the initiative is and the context around it.

What is known:
- There is a project that needs a pre-mortem

What must be confirmed:
- What is the initiative? (what is being built, changed, or launched)
- What is the scope and timeline?
- What are the key dependencies or risks already known?
- Who is the team and what constraints do they have?

Without these, the pre-mortem will produce generic failure modes that apply to any project — not the specific failure modes that matter for this one.

The value of a pre-mortem is in surfacing specific, realistic failures for a specific plan. Generic failure modes like "we might be delayed" or "stakeholders might disagree" are not useful.

Suggested next inputs:
- Initiative description and scope
- Timeline and team
- Key dependencies
- Known concerns or constraints

Confidence level:
Provisional
