## Input
Map our acquisition funnel.

## Expected output
Funnel mapping requires knowing what product is being analyzed and having at least rough data on how users move through it.

What is known:
- An acquisition funnel needs to be mapped

What must be provided:
- What is the product and how does it acquire users?
- What is the activation event?
- Are any funnel conversion rates or volumes available?

Without these, the output is a generic funnel stage template (awareness → signup → activation → retained) that describes the concept, not your funnel.

Suggested next inputs:
- Product type and main acquisition channels
- Rough signup volume and activation rate if available
- Activation event definition

Confidence level:
Provisional
