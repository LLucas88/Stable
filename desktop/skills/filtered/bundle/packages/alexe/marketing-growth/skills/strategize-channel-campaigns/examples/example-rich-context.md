## Input
We're a B2C habit tracker app (iOS/Android, freemium). Current channels and performance:
- Meta Ads: 55% of new installs. CAC $18. Day-30 retention for Meta users: 14%.
- App Store Organic: 30% of installs. CAC $0. Day-30 retention: 26%.
- Influencer/YouTube: 15% of installs. CAC $32. Day-30 retention: 31%.

We're spending $45k/month on paid. Free-to-paid conversion is 4.1%. Our OKR: reduce blended CAC by 20% while maintaining new user volume.

## Expected output
Current channel assessment:

| Channel | Install share | CAC | Day-30 retention | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta Ads | 55% | $18 | 14% | High volume, low quality |
| App Store Organic | 30% | $0 | 26% | High quality, naturally limited by ASO and ratings |
| Influencer/YouTube | 15% | $32 | 31% | Highest quality, high cost, not scalable quickly |

Channel-market fit evaluation:
- Meta Ads: poor fit for long-term retention. The 14% Day-30 retention vs. 26% organic suggests the paid audience is significantly lower intent. At 4.1% free-to-paid conversion, Meta users are converting to paid at a rate that makes the CAC math marginal (need to model full LTV to confirm).
- App Store Organic: excellent fit. Highest quality, zero CAC. The constraint is that organic growth requires investment in ASO, ratings/reviews, and content — not just spend.
- Influencer/YouTube: highest retention and best quality, but $32 CAC limits scale. ROI depends on LTV; if LTV exceeds $96 (3x CAC), it's worth scaling.

Channel portfolio gaps and risks:
- Over-concentration in Meta (55%): algorithm changes or iOS tracking restrictions can wipe out the majority of install volume overnight
- No SEO or content channel: for a habit tracker, there's a large organic search audience for terms like "habit tracker app" or "daily routine app" — currently not captured
- No referral program: word of mouth is untapped despite having a product type (habits) that users naturally discuss with friends

Experimentation plan:

| Channel to test | Hypothesis | Success criteria |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok Ads | Younger demographics may have better retention fit than Meta | CAC <$22 with Day-30 retention >18% |
| Content / SEO (blog) | Organic content targeting habit-related search terms can reduce paid dependency | 5%+ of new installs from organic content within 6 months |
| Referral program | Current users sharing the app with friends will produce higher-retention installs at lower effective CAC | Referral CAC <$10, referral user Day-30 retention >20% |

Recommended channel prioritization:
1. Invest in App Store Organic (ASO, ratings strategy, app quality) — the highest ROI investment given zero CAC and best retention. Incremental organic growth from 30% to 40% of installs frees up $13.5k/month in paid spend at current volume.
2. Test referral program (low cost to launch) — if referral users retain similarly to organic, this is a near-zero CAC acquisition channel.
3. Test TikTok as a Meta diversification move — reduce single-platform risk.
4. Reduce Meta spend by 15-20% and reallocate to ASO and referral — this is the mechanical path to the 20% blended CAC reduction.

Budget allocation principles:
- Reallocate, don't just add: reducing Meta from 55% to 40% of installs while growing organic from 30% to 40% achieves a blended CAC reduction without cutting volume.
- Test budgets for new channels should be time-boxed (4-6 weeks minimum for statistical validity) and sized to produce at least 200 attributed installs.

Monitoring cadence:
- Weekly: CAC by channel, install volume by channel
- Monthly: Day-30 retention by acquisition cohort and channel
- Quarterly: blended CAC trend vs. OKR target
