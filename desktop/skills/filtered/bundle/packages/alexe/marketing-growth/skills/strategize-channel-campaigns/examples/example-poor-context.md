## Input
We need a channel strategy. What channels should we use?

## Expected output
Channel strategy requires knowing the product, the target audience, and current performance.

What is known:
- A channel strategy is needed

What must be confirmed:
- What is the product and target user? (B2B vs. B2C, audience segment)
- What channels are currently in use and what is their performance? (CAC, conversion rate)
- What is the CAC target or budget range?
- What is the acquisition goal? (volume, quality, diversification)

Without knowing the audience and current baseline, any channel recommendation is untethered speculation.

Suggested next inputs:
- Product type and target audience
- Current channels and rough performance
- Budget or CAC target

Confidence level:
Provisional
