## Input
What growth loops does my product have?

## Expected output
Growth loops can't be identified without knowing what the product does and how it currently acquires users.

What is known:
- A product exists

What must be provided:
- What does the product do? (core user action and value proposition)
- How are users currently acquired? (paid, organic, referral, word of mouth)
- Any data on referral share or organic growth?

Different product types have different natural loop candidates:
- Marketplaces: supply-side content loops, buyer referral loops
- Social products: social sharing loops, network effect loops
- Content products: SEO content loops, creator supply loops
- Collaboration tools: team invitation loops

Without knowing the product type and current growth patterns, growth loop identification produces generic categories rather than actual loops embedded in this product.

Suggested next inputs:
- Product description and core user action
- Current growth channels (rough)
- Any existing referral or social sharing behavior

Confidence level:
Provisional
