Channel-market fit evaluation:
For B2B HR SaaS, content marketing and SEO are a strong conceptual fit:
- HR professionals search for specific problems (e.g., "how to reduce employee turnover," "onboarding checklist template") — high-intent organic traffic is achievable
- Content builds trust before sales contact — reduces cold outreach friction
- Timeline: content and SEO take 6-12 months to produce meaningful traffic, so this is a medium-term investment

Channel portfolio gaps and risks:
- Outbound-only or outbound-heavy creates pipeline variability — quota-dependent, hard to scale without headcount
- No current product-led or inbound motion means every new customer requires direct sales effort
- LinkedIn Ads CAC for B2B SaaS is typically high ($150-400+ per trial/demo) — worth measuring before scaling

Experimentation plan (draft):
- Content/SEO: 3-month pilot creating 8-10 SEO articles targeting high-intent HR search terms. Success criteria: 500+ organic visits/month by month 6.
- LinkedIn Ads: if not currently measured, add CAC tracking before deciding to scale or cut.

Recommended channel prioritization:
1. Define CAC and pipeline attribution for current channels before adding new ones — without baselines, it's impossible to benchmark new channel performance
2. Start content in parallel if headcount is available — it's low-risk but requires patience
3. Don't cut outbound while content/SEO is unproven — bridge the transition
