## Input
We have a recipe app, freemium. We get most users from Instagram and TikTok. Conversion to premium is low. Users save recipes and follow chefs. Not sure how to think about our growth model.

## Expected output
Growth funnel map with metrics at each stage (placeholders — data needed):

| Stage | Metric | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | New installs/month | Unknown — Instagram and TikTok are the primary sources |
| Activation | First recipe saved / first chef followed | Activation event not confirmed |
| Retention | Day-7 / Day-30 return rate | Unknown |
| Referral | Shares / referral-driven installs | Unknown |
| Revenue | Free-to-paid conversion | "Low" (baseline unknown) |

Primary growth loop description:
Candidate loops based on product type:
1. Content loop (most likely): user saves recipes → uses the app → chef publishes → content discovery attracts more users. This is common for content-heavy apps with creator supply.
2. Social loop (possible): users follow chefs → chef content drives app opens → satisfied users share content externally → new installs. This requires sharing to be an intentional product action, not passive.
3. Paid acquisition (current): Instagram/TikTok paid (or organic) → installs. This is linear, not compounding.

Stage-level assessment:
Unknown for most stages — numbers aren't available. The fact that "conversion is low" without a baseline makes it impossible to assess how low and what should change.

Leverage analysis:
Cannot identify highest-leverage stage without retention and activation data. Common pattern for content apps: activation is the bottleneck (users install after seeing a recipe, open the app, don't understand what to do next, and leave). This is worth checking first.

Recommended growth priorities:
1. Establish baseline metrics: pull activation rate, Day-7 and Day-30 retention, and free-to-paid conversion rate before deciding where to focus
2. Once activation and retention are known, assess whether the content loop is working — are users who engage with recipes and follow chefs retaining better than those who don't?

Data gaps:
- Activation event undefined (saving a recipe vs. following a chef have different retention implications)
- No retention data
- No conversion rate baseline
- Traffic volume and channel breakdown unknown

Confidence level:
Provisional — a growth model framework is sketched, but almost every stage needs data before a real assessment is possible.
