## Input
Help me build a growth model for my product.

## Expected output
A growth model requires knowing what the product does, how it acquires users, and some data on current performance.

What is known:
- A product exists that needs a growth model

What must be confirmed before starting:
- What is the product? (type, value proposition, target user)
- What is the business model? (subscription, transactional, ad-supported, usage-based)
- How are users currently acquired? (paid channels, organic, referral, SEO)
- What rough data exists? (even estimates for activation and retention)

Without these inputs, the output is a generic AARRR framework that describes the structure of growth models in general — not your growth model.

Suggested next inputs:
- Product description and business model
- Primary acquisition channels and rough volume
- Any available data on activation, retention, or conversion

Confidence level:
Provisional
