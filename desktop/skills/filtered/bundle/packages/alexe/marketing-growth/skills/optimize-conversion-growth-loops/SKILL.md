# Optimize Conversion & Growth Loops

## Purpose
Help teams diagnose conversion bottlenecks and design compounding growth loops that amplify product-led or network-effect growth.

## Skill type
Conceptual skill

## Use this skill when
- Conversion rates at key funnel steps are below potential
- A growth loop exists but is not being reinforced
- A product has viral or referral potential that isn't being leveraged
- A paywall or upgrade conversion needs improvement

## Do not use this skill when
- The goal is top-of-funnel channel acquisition (use strategize-channel-campaigns)
- The goal is lifecycle engagement design (use plan-lifecycle-engagement)

## Required inputs
- Conversion metric or flow to optimize
- Current conversion data (even rough)

## Optional inputs
- Funnel analysis
- User research on friction points
- A/B test history
- Growth loop map

## Upstream context
Works best when:
- Growth model is defined
- Funnel data is available
- Growth loop hypothesis exists

## Downstream handoff
Output can feed:
- formulate-experiment-hypothesis (conversion improvement idea → experiment)
- design-growth-model (strengthened loops update the growth model)

## Instructions
1. Identify the conversion event being optimized.
2. Map the current conversion flow step by step.
3. Identify friction points, drop-off steps, and barriers.
4. Identify the growth loop: what action creates a reinforcing cycle?
5. Propose conversion improvements and loop reinforcement mechanisms.
6. Prioritize interventions by estimated impact and implementation effort.

## Output
Provide:
- Conversion flow map
- Friction points and drop-off analysis
- Growth loop description (if applicable)
- Optimization recommendations by priority
- Loop reinforcement mechanisms
- Measurement plan for each recommendation

## Risks / caveats
- Optimizing a broken growth loop faster still leads to failure
- Dark patterns improve short-term conversion at the cost of trust — avoid them
- Always measure long-term retention impact of conversion changes, not just conversion rate
