## Input
We want to test influencer marketing for our app. We haven't done it before. Not sure what budget to use or how to measure success.

## Expected output
Channel hypothesis (draft):
Influencer marketing hypothesis requires defining which influencers, which segment, and why influencer audiences should convert for this product. Without that, the hypothesis is too generic to test rigorously.

A starting hypothesis template: "We believe partnering with [type of influencer] will acquire [target user segment] at a CAC under [X] because [reason their audience has high intent for our product]."

To complete: confirm the product type, the target audience, and why influencer audiences are likely to match.

Success criteria:
Cannot be defined without knowing current CAC baseline. Influencer campaign success should be measured against the cost per install or cost per activated user from existing channels.

Failure criteria:
Similarly need a baseline. A common mistake in first influencer tests: the test budget is too small to generate a meaningful number of installs, so results are inconclusive by design.

Minimum test budget and timeline:
For influencer marketing, a meaningful test typically requires:
- At least 2-3 influencers to reduce single-creator variance
- Tracking link per creator (to attribute installs)
- Enough installs to measure quality (retention / conversion), not just volume

Budget guidance without data: micro-influencer campaigns ($5k-$15k) are a reasonable starting point for a first test. But "not sure what budget to use" suggests the team hasn't yet defined what outcome they'd need to see to consider the test a success.

Measurement plan:
Required tracking:
- Unique UTM or tracking link per creator
- App install attribution per creator source
- Post-install activation and retention tracked by acquisition cohort

Missing information to design the experiment properly:
- What is the product and target user?
- What are existing channel CAC and user quality benchmarks?
- What budget is available?

Risks and mitigations:
- Measuring influencer campaigns on volume alone (views, mentions) without install tracking produces no actionable learning.

Confidence level:
Provisional
