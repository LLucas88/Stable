## Input
Our conversion is low. How do we fix it?

## Expected output
Conversion optimization requires knowing what conversion is being measured and where users are dropping out.

What is known:
- Conversion is low

What must be confirmed:
- What conversion event is being measured? (signup to active, free to paid, trial to subscription, etc.)
- What is the current conversion rate and what would "good" look like?
- What is the conversion flow? (the steps between the start and the conversion event)
- Where in the flow are users dropping off?

Without a defined conversion event and funnel data, any recommendations will be generic best practices, not a diagnosis of your specific bottleneck.

Suggested next inputs:
- Conversion metric and current rate
- Funnel steps between start and conversion
- Drop-off data by step (even rough estimates)

Confidence level:
Provisional
