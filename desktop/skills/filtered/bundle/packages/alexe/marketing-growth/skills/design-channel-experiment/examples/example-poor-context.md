## Input
We want to try a new channel. How do we set up the experiment?

## Expected output
A channel experiment requires knowing which channel is being tested and why, before the design can start.

What is known:
- The team wants to test a new channel

What must be confirmed:
- Which channel? (TikTok, influencer, podcast ads, SEO, etc.)
- Why do you believe this channel can acquire your users? (the hypothesis)
- What is the current channel baseline for comparison? (existing CAC, retention)
- What is the target metric for success?

Without a channel hypothesis, any experiment structure is just a spending framework with no expected outcome.

Suggested next inputs:
- Channel to test
- Why this channel should work for your product and audience
- Budget available
- Baseline channel metrics for comparison

Confidence level:
Provisional
