# 📈 Marketing & Growth

[← Back to all domains](../README.md)

Skills for designing growth models, mapping acquisition funnels, building lifecycle engagement, running channel experiments, and optimizing conversion and growth loops.

Growth isn't just "more users." It's understanding why users come, when they activate, what makes them stay, and how that cycle compounds. This domain gives your agent the frameworks to model your growth engine, find the leverage points, and design experiments that test real channel and conversion hypotheses — not just throw money at ads.

## 🛠️ Skills in this domain

### 1. `design-growth-model`

Use this when the team doesn't have a clear growth model, growth has plateaued, or a new product needs its growth engine designed.

What it does:
- Maps the growth funnel: acquisition → activation → retention → referral → revenue
- Identifies conversion rates at each stage
- Finds where a 10% improvement has the biggest downstream impact
- Identifies the primary growth loop and biggest gap

---

### 2. `map-acquisition-funnel`

Use this when acquisition brings users in but activation is low, or the team lacks a shared view of the funnel.

What it does:
- Defines stages: awareness → interest → signup → onboarding → activation → retained
- Maps conversion rates between stages, by channel if possible
- Identifies biggest conversion gaps (volume × drop-off, not just rate)
- Prioritizes 1–2 stages for improvement

---

### 3. `identify-growth-loop`

Use this when the team relies entirely on paid acquisition with no compounding growth, or organic growth exists but isn't understood.

What it does:
- Maps candidate loops: acquisition, engagement, content, network effect
- Describes each cycle and estimates current strength
- Identifies weakest link in each active loop
- Identifies loops that should exist but don't

---

### 4. `optimize-conversion-growth-loops`

Use this when conversion rates at key funnel steps are below potential, or a growth loop exists but isn't being reinforced.

What it does:
- Maps current conversion flow step by step
- Identifies friction points, drop-off steps, and barriers
- Maps the growth loop and its reinforcing mechanism
- Proposes improvements prioritized by impact and effort

---

### 5. `design-onboarding-flow`

Use this when new user activation rates are low, users sign up but don't complete onboarding, or a new product needs onboarding designed.

What it does:
- Defines the activation moment (what signals core value was experienced)
- Maps the critical path: minimum steps to reach activation
- Defers everything that can wait until after activation
- Plans follow-up touchpoints for users who don't activate in session 1

---

### 6. `plan-lifecycle-engagement`

Use this when users are churning without engagement interventions, or no lifecycle messaging strategy exists.

What it does:
- Maps user lifecycle stages: new → activated → retained → at-risk → churned
- Designs touchpoints per stage: trigger, message, channel, goal
- Prioritizes lifecycle moments with highest retention impact
- Identifies automation opportunities

---

### 7. `strategize-channel-campaigns`

Use this when the team is unclear which channels to invest in, or growth relies on one channel and diversification is needed.

What it does:
- Maps current channel portfolio and performance
- Assesses channel-market fit per audience
- Identifies concentration risks
- Designs channel experimentation plan with hypotheses and success criteria

---

### 8. `design-channel-experiment`

Use this when a new channel is being considered and needs a structured test before scaling.

What it does:
- States channel hypothesis: channel, segment, expected CAC/conversion
- Defines success/failure criteria before launch
- Designs test parameters with minimum viable budget
- Builds decision framework: scale / iterate / kill triggers

## 🧭 How to use this domain

### Typical paths:

**Building the growth engine:**
1. `design-growth-model` → understand where you are
2. `map-acquisition-funnel` → find the biggest gaps
3. `identify-growth-loop` → find compounding opportunities
4. `optimize-conversion-growth-loops` → fix and reinforce

**Improving new user activation:**
1. `map-acquisition-funnel` → identify activation gap
2. `design-onboarding-flow` → redesign the path to value
3. `plan-lifecycle-engagement` → catch users who don't activate

**Testing new channels:**
1. `strategize-channel-campaigns` → prioritize channels
2. `design-channel-experiment` → run structured tests

**Enter in the middle:**
- Activation is low → start with `design-onboarding-flow`
- Paid only, no organic growth → start with `identify-growth-loop`
- Need to test a channel → start with `design-channel-experiment`
- Users churn after month 1 → start with `plan-lifecycle-engagement`

## ⚠️ What this domain protects against

Common growth failure modes:

- **Growth without retention.** Pouring users into a leaky bucket. `design-growth-model` flags that retention is the first lever, not acquisition.
- **Scaling unproven channels.** Spending $100K on a channel tested at $500. `design-channel-experiment` requires proving unit economics at small scale first.
- **Dark patterns for conversion.** Short-term wins that destroy trust. `optimize-conversion-growth-loops` measures long-term retention impact, not just conversion rate.
- **Onboarding that asks before it delivers.** Signup forms before value. `design-onboarding-flow` defers data collection until after the activation moment.
- **Email blasting as lifecycle strategy.** Messages without behavioral triggers are spam. `plan-lifecycle-engagement` ties every touchpoint to a trigger and a goal.
