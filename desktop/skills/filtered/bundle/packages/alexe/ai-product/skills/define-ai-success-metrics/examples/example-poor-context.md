## Input
What metrics should I track for an AI feature?

## Expected output
AI success metrics cannot be defined without knowing what the AI feature does.

What is needed:
- AI feature description: what does the AI do? What input does it take, what output does it produce?
- Target user: who uses this feature and for what goal?
- Business goal: what business outcome should this feature affect?

Without these inputs, any metrics listed would be a generic AI metric checklist — not a measurement framework for your specific feature.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The AI feature [describes what it does] for [user segment]"
- "The business goal is [retention / support deflection / revenue / efficiency]"
- "Failure would look like [what goes wrong if the AI doesn't work]"
