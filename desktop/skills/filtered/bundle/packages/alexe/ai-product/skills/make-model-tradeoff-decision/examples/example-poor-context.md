## Input
Compare AI models for me.

## Expected output
A model trade-off comparison requires knowing what the models need to do and what constraints they're being evaluated against.

What is needed:
- The specific use case: what task will the model perform?
- At least 2 candidate models or approaches to compare
- Key requirements: what does the model need to do well? (Accuracy, cost, latency, privacy, etc.)

Without a use case and requirements, any model comparison would be a generic capability summary with no relevance to your actual decision.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The use case is [description — input, output, volume]"
- "I'm comparing [Model A] vs. [Model B] (or [approach A] vs. [approach B])"
- "The key constraints are [latency / cost / quality / privacy / other]"
