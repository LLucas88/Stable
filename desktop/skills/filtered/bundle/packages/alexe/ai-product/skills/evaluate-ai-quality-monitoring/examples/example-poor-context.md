## Input
How do I monitor AI quality?

## Expected output
AI quality monitoring design requires knowing what the AI feature does and what "bad quality" looks like for users.

What is needed:
- AI feature description: what does the AI produce? What does a user do with it?
- User-facing quality implications: if the AI output is wrong or bad, what happens to the user?

Without knowing the feature, generic quality dimensions like "accuracy" and "relevance" have no concrete meaning.

Suggested next inputs:
- "The AI feature [does this] and produces [output type]"
- "A bad AI output would [effect on the user]"
- "The feature has been in production for [time period] and we're seeing [issue / no issue yet]"
