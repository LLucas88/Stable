## Input
Give me AI feature ideas.

## Expected output
AI feature ideation requires a product context and a user problem to ideate around.

"Give me AI feature ideas" without any context would produce a generic list of AI features that could apply to any product — not features grounded in real user problems your product actually has.

What is needed:
- Product area or problem to address: what part of the product? What problem exists?
- User segment and key pain points: who has the problem? What do they struggle with today?

Without these inputs, any ideas generated would be disconnected from your actual users and their needs.

Suggested next inputs:
- "Our product is [description]. The problem users have is [pain point]."
- "We're looking to add AI in [product area] where users currently struggle with [specific friction]."
