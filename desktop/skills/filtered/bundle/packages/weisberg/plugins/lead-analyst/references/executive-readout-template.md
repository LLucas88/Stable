# Executive Readout Template

Use this template for `/lead-analyst:executive-readout`.

```markdown
# Executive Readout: <topic>

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Audience: <team / exec / board / operating review>
Status: Draft / Ready

## Decision
What decision should this readout help make?

## Recommendation
The recommended action in one paragraph.

## Confidence
High / Medium / Low, with one sentence explaining why.

## What Changed
The smallest useful summary of the movement, opportunity, risk, or result.

## Evidence
| Evidence | What It Shows | Caveat |
|----------|---------------|--------|

## So What
Business, product, customer, operational, or financial consequence.

## Options
| Option | Upside | Downside | When To Choose |
|--------|--------|----------|----------------|

## Risks And Caveats
Only caveats that could change the decision.

## Next Action
Owner, action, and date.

## Appendix
Definitions, source notes, quality checks, and detailed cuts.
```
