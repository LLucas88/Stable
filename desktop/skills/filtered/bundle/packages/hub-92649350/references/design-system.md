# Editorial-grid motion design system

## Base style

- Background: warm ivory `#f8f4eb`, with a restrained editorial grid.
- Primary text: charcoal `#171717`.
- Accent red: `#e94733`.
- Accent blue: `#1b5cb8`.
- Accent yellow: `#efb83d`.
- Chinese font: a normal locally available sans-serif such as Microsoft YaHei or Noto Sans CJK.

The result should feel like an editorial infographic in motion: clear hierarchy, physical state changes, limited color, and generous spacing.

## Typography

- Main title starting point: 80–94px, line-height 1.12–1.2, natural letter spacing, maximum width about 850px.
- Supporting text: 26–44px depending on hierarchy.
- Avoid more than three title lines. When title and illustration compete, reduce copy or use a controlled two-line title; never squeeze Chinese glyphs.
- Do not rely on tiny labels to carry the core meaning.

## Safe zones

For 1080×1920 cross-platform delivery: left ≥72px, right ≥180px, top ≥160px, bottom ≥300px, main illustration bottom ≤1450px, and captions in an isolated band at bottom 300px.

## Captions

- White text on a dark semi-opaque rectangle.
- Starting size: 32–36px.
- Padding: approximately 14px vertical and 24px horizontal.
- Optional restrained white border and blue offset shadow.
- `white-space: nowrap`; one cue, one line.
- Center horizontally inside the safe width, not across the full canvas.

## Motion language

Each scene uses **setup → change → reveal**. Good motion includes counters, progress changes, mechanisms that expose a result, workflow assembly, state transitions, and conclusion stamps. Avoid decorative bounce, glow-only activity, mechanical shot changes, static image slideshows, and repeated card entrances.

## Cover

The cover is an independent 3:4 design. Use one clear hook, one visual metaphor, and no more than one supporting line. Test at phone-thumbnail size. Do not use a cropped video screenshot.
